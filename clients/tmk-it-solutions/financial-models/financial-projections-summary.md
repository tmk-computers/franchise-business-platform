## 2. Key Insights for Analyst 2
1. **Solvency in Ramp-Up:** Under the conservative scenario, the franchisee incurs an initial EBITDA deficit during the 5-month sales ramp. The ₹1,40,000 working capital reserve ensures the office remains solvent without capital distress.
2. **The Power of Compounded Renewals:** In Years 2 and 3, recurring annual renewal commissions account for over 35% of total franchisee EBITDA, demonstrating the structural superiority of the software franchise model over one-time product sales.
3. **Franchisor Royalty Health:** TMK's 60% retained revenue generates significant recurring gross contributions, fully covering central software R&D, cloud server provisioning, and dedicated technical implementation staff.
