# FRANCHISE DEFAULT & TERMINATION POLICY (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Events of Default
The following events constitute material default by Franchisee:
1. Failure to maintain a physical commercial office or failure to employ at least one certified salesperson.
2. Unlawful solicitation of prospects outside the designated territory or unauthorized discounting.
3. Attempting to reverse engineer, decompile, copy, or distribute TMK software source code.
4. Insolvency, bankruptcy, or failure to cure performance distress following a 60-day Remedial Cure Notice.

---

## 2. Notice and 30-Day Cure Period
Prior to terminating for operational default, Franchisor shall issue a formal written **Notice of Default**. Franchisee shall have thirty (30) calendar days from receipt of notice to remedy the cited deficiency.

---

## 3. Post-Termination Covenants
Upon termination:
1. Franchisee shall immediately cease using TMK trademarks, logos, marketing collateral, and software demonstration sandboxes.
2. Franchisee shall remove all exterior and interior branded signage within fourteen (14) days.
3. All active customer accounts and renewal rights revert 100% to Franchisor.
4. Franchisee shall be subject to a twelve (12) month non-solicitation covenant regarding existing TMK customers within the territory.
