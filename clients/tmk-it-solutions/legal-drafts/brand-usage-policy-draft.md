# BRAND USAGE & TRADEMARK POLICY (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Permitted Usage of Trademark
Franchisee is granted a limited, revocable, non-exclusive license to use the trademark **"TMK IT Solutions"** and associated product logos solely in conjunction with identifying the local office as an:
> *"Authorized Franchise Partner of TMK IT Solutions LLP"*

Franchisee shall NOT represent itself as the corporate owner, developer, or legal parent entity of TMK IT Solutions LLP.

---

## 2. Signage and Advertising Guidelines
1. **Office Signage:** All exterior boards, reception logos, and window vinyls must adhere strictly to TMK brand color codes (Navy Blue `#1F497D`, Orange `#E36C0A`) and font typography.
2. **Pre-Approval of Local Advertising:** Any newspaper advertisements, outdoor hoardings, or social media campaigns created by Franchisee must be submitted to TMK central marketing for written brand approval prior to publication.
3. **Domain Names & Social Media Handles:** Franchisee shall not register internet domain names, social media profile handles, or trademarks containing the name "TMK", "TMK IT Solutions", or specific product names without express written authorization.
