# NON-DISCLOSURE & CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" includes all software architecture, database schemas, pricing strategies, unannounced product features, customer prospect registers, commission calculations, and operational training manuals disclosed by Franchisor to Franchisee.

---

## 2. Nondisclosure Obligations
1. Franchisee agrees to hold all Confidential Information in strict trust and confidence, exercising no less than a reasonable degree of care.
2. Franchisee shall disclose Confidential Information only to employees who have a legitimate need to know for the operation of the franchise and who have signed confidentiality obligations.
3. Franchisee shall not use Confidential Information for the development, marketing, or sale of any competing software products.

---

## 3. Term of Confidentiality
The obligations under this Agreement shall survive the termination or expiration of the franchise relationship for a period of five (5) years, except for trade secrets and software source code, which shall remain confidential indefinitely.
