### ARTICLE 1: GRANT OF FRANCHISE & TERRITORY
1.1. Franchisor hereby grants to Franchisee, and Franchisee accepts, the non-transferable, exclusive commercial right to market, demonstrate, and promote the designated software products within the designated administrative Taluka: ______________________, District: ______________________, State: ______________________ ("Designated Territory").
1.2. Franchisee acknowledges that this grant confers commercial representation rights and does NOT transfer ownership of underlying software copyrights, trademarks, or intellectual property.

---

### ARTICLE 2: FEES & FINANCIAL SETTLEMENT
2.1. **Joining Fee:** Franchisee shall pay an upfront joining fee of ₹_______________ (+18% GST) upon execution of this Agreement.
2.2. **Revenue Share:** Franchisee shall receive a 40% (forty percent) commission on Net Realized Revenues for all new software licenses and annual subscription renewals originated within the Designated Territory.
2.3. **Payment Terms:** Franchisor shall calculate and remit commissions monthly by the 10th day of the subsequent calendar month.

---

### ARTICLE 3: FRANCHISEE OBLIGATIONS & MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS
3.1. Franchisee shall establish and maintain at its own expense a dedicated commercial office space of not less than 200 sq. ft.
3.2. Franchisee shall employ at least one (1) dedicated, full-time sales executive exclusively assigned to software sales.
3.3. Franchisee shall ensure its sales personnel complete Franchisor's mandatory 10-day Product Certification Bootcamp.

---

### ARTICLE 4: FRANCHISOR OBLIGATIONS & SUPPORT
4.1. Franchisor shall be solely responsible for software development, cloud hosting uptime, database backups, bug fixes, and customer implementation.
4.2. Franchisor shall provide Level-2 and Level-3 technical helpdesk services to end customers.

---

### ARTICLE 5: TERM & TERMINATION
5.1. **Term:** The initial term of this Agreement shall be three (3) years from the Effective Date.
5.2. **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate upon thirty (30) days written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure such breach within the 30-day cure period.
