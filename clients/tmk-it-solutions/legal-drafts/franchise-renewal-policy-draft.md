# FRANCHISE RENEWAL POLICY (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Term of Renewal
Upon expiration of the initial three (3) year term, the Franchisee shall have the option to renew for an additional three (3) year term.

---

## 2. Renewal Qualification Criteria
To qualify for renewal without penalty, Franchisee must satisfy:
1. **Sales Performance:** Achievement of at least 70% of cumulative territory sales quotas over the 3-year term.
2. **Operational Compliance:** A passing score ($\ge 80\%$) on the most recent annual brand and operational audit.
3. **Good Financial Standing:** Zero unresolved commission chargebacks or unpaid fee balances.
4. **Staff Certification:** Verification that active sales personnel hold current TMK product certifications.
