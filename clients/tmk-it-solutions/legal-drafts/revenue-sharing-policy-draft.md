# REVENUE SHARING & SETTLEMENT POLICY (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Scope & Definitions
- **Net Realized Revenue:** Gross invoice amount received from customer minus statutory taxes (18% GST).
- **Settlement Cycle:** Monthly calendar reconciliation covering the 1st through the final day of each calendar month.

---

## 2. Commission Entitlement
1. **New Software Licenses:** Franchisee is entitled to 40% of Net Realized Revenue on all new customer contracts closed within the designated territory.
2. **Annual Renewals:** Franchisee is entitled to 40% of Net Realized Revenue on all annual software maintenance subscription renewals, provided the customer remains active and the franchisee maintains local relationship support.
3. **Implementation Services:** For customer contracts requiring specialized on-site or remote implementation by TMK central engineering, the implementation service charge is split: 80% retained by TMK and 20% disbursed to the franchisee.

---

## 3. Dispute Resolution & Reversals
If a customer exercises a contractual refund or chargeback, the commission paid to the franchisee for that contract shall be reversed and debited against the franchisee's subsequent monthly settlement.
