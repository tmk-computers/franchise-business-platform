# TERRITORY ALLOCATION & EXCLUSIVITY POLICY (WORKING DRAFT)

> [!CAUTION]
> **DISCLAIMER:** Non-final working draft for commercial modeling purposes. Not formal legal advice.

---

## 1. Territorial Boundaries
Territories are granted strictly based on official Indian Government sub-district administrative boundaries (**Taluka / Tehsil**).

---

## 2. Deal Registration & Anti-Poaching
1. Franchisees must log prospective customer leads in TMK's central CRM.
2. An authorized lead entry creates an exclusive **90-Day Deal Lock**. Adjacent franchisees or corporate sales teams may not solicit a locked account.
3. If an account is not closed within 90 days, the lock expires unless an extension is granted based on verified proof of active procurement negotiations.

---

## 3. Cross-Territory Accounts
For multi-branch enterprise customers spanning multiple talukas, revenue shall be apportioned: 60% to the territory originating the corporate purchasing decision, and 40% shared among territories providing local deployment support.
