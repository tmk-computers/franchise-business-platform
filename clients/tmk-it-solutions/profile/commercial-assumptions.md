## 2. Critical Revenue Sharing Nuances & Exclusions

1. **Treatment of Statutory Taxes (GST):**
   The proposed 40:60 split **DOES NOT apply to GST**. All customer billings are subject to statutory Indian GST (typically 18%). GST collected from customers is a statutory liability remitted 100% to tax authorities by TMK IT Solutions LLP as the invoicing entity. The 40% commission is computed strictly on the **Net Realized Amount**.
2. **Treatment of Implementation & Data Migration Charges:**
   Where TMK's central technical engineering team executes on-site/remote data migration and system configuration, the 40:60 split does not apply automatically. The proposed treatment is 80% retained by TMK (delivery cost recovery) and 20% to the franchisee (local coordination).
3. **Third-Party Infrastructure & API Consumables:**
   Variable third-party pass-through expenses (such as WhatsApp Business API messaging fees, transactional SMS gateway credits, or custom cloud storage overages) are either billed to the customer at cost or deducted prior to margin distribution.
4. **Subscription Renewals:**
   The 40% franchisee share for Year 2 and Year 3 renewals is conditional upon the franchisee maintaining active local relationship support and achieving a territory customer retention rate $\ge 80\%$.
5. **Customer Cancellations & Chargebacks:**
   In the event of a customer contract cancellation and refund during the statutory warranty period, franchisee commissions previously disbursed for that transaction are subject to full clawback against future monthly settlements.

## 3. Open Management Decisions Requiring TMK Leadership Sign-Off

The following decisions are logged in `clients/tmk-it-solutions/decisions/management-decision-register.md` and require executive resolution:

- **Decision DEC-001 (Product Bundling):** Will franchisees be permitted to purchase franchise rights for a single standalone product (e.g., School ERP only for ₹25,000), or will TMK mandate a minimum 3-product or 6-product bundle?
- **Decision DEC-002 (Renewal Commission Duration):** Will the 40% renewal commission persist in perpetuity for the duration of the 3-year agreement, or transition to a stepped-down maintenance tier (e.g., 25%) in Year 3?
- **Decision DEC-003 (Territory Exclusivity Threshold):** What minimum quarterly sales volume must a franchisee achieve to preserve territorial exclusivity within their designated Taluka?
- **Decision DEC-004 (Direct Central Inbound Leads):** How will inbound customer inquiries originating from TMK corporate marketing be credited if the customer is located in an assigned franchisee taluka?
