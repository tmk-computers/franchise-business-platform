# TMK IT Solutions LLP: 10-Day Franchisee Training Curriculum

**Document ID:** `TMK-OPS-003`  
**Status:** `[DRAFT - OPERATIONAL SPEC]`  
**Target:** Onboarding certified commercial partners across Maharashtra Talukas

---

## Program Overview
The TMK Franchisee Onboarding Training Program prepares new franchise operators to master TMK's 6 enterprise software products, operate the partner CRM portal, conduct compelling demonstrations, and manage compliant unit operations under the FOFO model.

---

## 10-Day Training Schedule

### Day 1: Corporate Mission, B2B Software Market & FOFO Model
- TMK IT Solutions LLP company history, leadership vision, and commercial ethos.
- Understanding the Tier-2/3 Indian software market opportunity (accounting, schools, clinics).
- The FOFO Operating Model: Franchisor central delivery vs. Franchisee local relationship responsibilities.

### Day 2: Enterprise ERP & Invoicing Platform Mastery
- In-depth navigation of Cloud Enterprise ERP (`prd-01`) and Invoicing App+Web (`prd-02`).
- Setting up chart of accounts, GST invoicing, inventory tracking, and multi-user permissions.
- Practical lab: creating test company, generating sales invoices, and running GST returns.

### Day 3: School Management & Healthcare ERP Mastery
- Deep dive into School & College Management Suite (`prd-03`) and Hospital ERP (`prd-04`).
- Student fee collection, attendance biometric integration, examination grade sheets.
- Patient OPD/IPD billing, pharmacy inventory, and electronic medical records.

### Day 4: Retail POS, Property Management & Technical Suites
- Retail POS & Multi-Store Inventory (`prd-05`) barcode scanning and receipt printers.
- Property & Society Management ERP (`prd-06`) maintenance billing and tenant registers.
- High-level overview of DevOps Automation and Cybersecurity Audit services.

### Day 5: Sales Methodologies, 5-Step Demo Script & Objections
- Executing the standard 5-step customer software demonstration.
- Positioning TMK against Tally, Marg ERP, and Teachmint.
- Handling price resistance, legacy data migration questions, and internet outage concerns.

### Day 6: CRM Operations, Lead Registration & Territory Governance
- Accessing the central partner portal; registering enterprise sales opportunities.
- The 90-day Deal Registration Lock: rules of engagement and anti-cannibalization protections.
- Generating formal commercial proposals and end-user license agreements.

### Day 7: Unit Launch, Office Setup & Statutory Record Keeping
- Setting up the local franchise office: signage, workstation layout, broadband backup.
- Statutory business licenses (Shop Act, Udyam MSME, GSTIN).
- Brand identity compliance: approved logos, visiting cards, email signatures.

### Day 8: Financial Reconciliation, Billing & 18% GST Segregation
- Calculating Net Realized Revenue and understanding the 40:60 split.
- Statutory GST pass-through handling and TDS deduction under Section 194H/J.
- Maintaining the unit's local operating expense budget and cash flow monitoring.

### Day 9: Customer Support Escalations & Central SLAs
- First-line (L1) customer problem triage and ticketing procedures.
- Central Tier-2/Tier-3 engineering escalation paths and SLA timelines.
- Customer satisfaction reviews, annual renewal retainers, and upsell workflows.

### Day 10: Mock Sales Demo, Certification Exam & Induction
- 45-minute live mock sales demonstration evaluated by TMK master trainer.
- 100-question written theory assessment (passing grade: 80%).
- Awarding of **Certified TMK Franchise Partner** credentials and welcome kit handover.
