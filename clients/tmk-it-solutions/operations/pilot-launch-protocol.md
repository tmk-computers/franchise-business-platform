# TMK IT Solutions LLP: Pilot Franchise Launch & Audit Protocol

**Document ID:** `TMK-OPS-004`  
**Status:** `[DRAFT - OPERATIONAL SPEC]`  
**Target:** Wave 1 Pilot Deployment across 3 Maharashtra Territories (Pune, Satara, Baramati)

---

## 1. Pilot Program Scope & Parameters
TMK IT Solutions LLP will deploy **Wave 1 Pilot Franchises** in three selected commercial catchments to validate the FOFO commercial model before opening statewide recruitment:
- **Unit 1 (Mature Urban / Tier-2):** Pune City (Haveli Taluka)
- **Unit 2 (Agricultural & Educational Regional Hub / Tier-3):** Baramati Taluka
- **Unit 3 (Developing Industrial Center / Tier-3):** Satara Taluka

---

## 2. Commercial Terms for Pilot Units
- **Promotional Joining Fee:** ₹25,000 (discounted from standard ₹50,000 for early adopters).
- **Revenue Sharing:** 40% Franchisee : 60% TMK IT Solutions LLP (on Net Realized Revenue).
- **Working Capital Reserve Verified:** Minimum ₹50,000 unencumbered cash balance.
- **Reporting Obligation:** Weekly pipeline log submission and bi-weekly review with TMK Senior Business Analyst.

---

## 3. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Evaluation Gates

### 30-Day Evaluation Checkpoint
- All 3 pilot offices branded, operational, and connected.
- Certified operators active; minimum 60 target accounts logged in CRM per unit.
- Minimum 20 live customer software demonstrations completed per unit.
- *Gate Clear Criterion:* Zero critical operational blockers; pipeline verified.

### 60-Day Evaluation Checkpoint
- Minimum 2–3 active software contracts signed per pilot territory.
- Central deployment SLA achieved: software instances provisioned within 48 hours of contract receipt.
- Billing and GST reconciliation executed with zero partner commission calculation disputes.
- *Gate Clear Criterion:* First revenue distribution executed cleanly.

### 90-Day Evaluation Checkpoint
- Cumulative sales reach 6–9 contracts per unit (tracking Base Case trajectory of 3 units/month).
- End-client customer satisfaction (CSAT) survey returns $\ge 85\%$ positive rating.
- Monthly franchisee EBITDA achieves positive operational cash flow.
- *Gate Clear Criterion:* Executive Committee signs off on **Gate 6 (Statewide Expansion Clearance)**.

---

## 4. Pilot Evaluation Governance Committee
- **Managing Partner:** Oversees overall commercial progress and partner sentiment.
- **Senior Business Analyst (Analyst 1):** Audits sales demos, pitch collateral effectiveness, and training feedback.
- **Quantitative Financial Analyst (Analyst 2):** Audits actual sales velocity, unit P&L, break-even progress, and commission statements.
- **Lead Software Architect:** Audits software uptime, cloud resource usage, bug reports, and deployment SLA adherence.
