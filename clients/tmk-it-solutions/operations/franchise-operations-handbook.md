# TMK Franchise Operations Handbook: Day-to-Day Operating Guide

## 1. Daily Operational Schedule
- **09:30 AM — Morning Standup:** Review daily demonstration calendar, prospect follow-ups, and CRM lead queue.
- **10:00 AM to 01:00 PM — Field Prospecting & In-Person Audits:** Salesperson conducts scheduled discovery meetings with school principals, trustees, and business owners.
- **02:00 PM to 04:30 PM — Co-Hosted Live Demonstrations:** Virtual product demonstrations co-hosted with TMK central solution architects.
- **04:30 PM to 06:00 PM — Proposal Generation & CRM Hygiene:** Logging call notes, issuing formal quotes via central software, and scheduling next-day visits.

---

## 2. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

### SOP-01: Logging a Qualified Lead
1. Open TMK central CRM app.
2. Enter institution name, decision-maker name, designation, mobile number, and student/entity size.
3. Verify that the address falls within the assigned Taluka code.
4. Submit lead entry. The system confirms an exclusive 90-day deal lock.

### SOP-02: Scheduling a Live Product Demonstration
1. Identify customer pain point (e.g., fee collection default for School ERP).
2. Schedule a 45-minute virtual demo slot at least 24 hours in advance via the central CRM scheduling calendar.
3. TMK central demo specialist receives calendar notification and joins the video conference 5 minutes prior to demo start.

### SOP-03: Closing a Deal & Order Handover
1. Generate standardized proposal quotation from the central portal.
2. Upon customer acceptance, collect customer payment via official TMK payment link or bank account.
3. Once funds clear, central billing automatically generates an official GST invoice and dispatches a welcome pack to the customer.
4. Central engineering initiates software instance provisioning within 24 hours.
