# TMK Technical Support & Service Level Agreement (SLA) Matrix

## 1. Issue Severity Classification & Response Targets

| Severity Level | Description & Example | Initial Response Time | Resolution / Workaround Target | Responsible Team |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Severity 1 (Critical)** | Entire software system down; school unable to record attendance or collect fees; database inaccessible. | **< 30 Minutes** | **< 4 Hours** | TMK Central Senior DevOps & Engineering Lead |
| **Severity 2 (Major)** | Core module malfunctioning (e.g., report card generation failing, or GST tax summary export error); no direct workaround. | **< 2 Hours** | **< 12 Hours** | TMK Central Product Engineering Team |
| **Severity 3 (Minor)** | Non-blocking bug, UI styling defect, or template layout request. | **< 6 Hours** | **< 48 Hours** | TMK Central Support Engineers |
| **Severity 4 (General Query)**| User password reset, how-to question, or feature enhancement suggestion. | **< 12 Hours** | **< 72 Hours** | Franchisee Level-1 Team with TMK Support |

---

## 2. Customer Implementation Timelines
- **School / College ERP:** Provisioned within 24 hours of payment verification; roster data imported and staff trained within 5 to 7 business days.
- **Invoicing App + Web:** Instant auto-provisioning upon online payment; catalog uploaded within 48 hours.
- **Property Management ERP:** Society ledger configured and guard app deployed within 5 business days.
