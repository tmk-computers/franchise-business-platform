# TMK Franchise Opportunity: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Business Concept & Role Clarification

### Q1: Do I need a computer science degree or software coding skills to own a TMK franchise?
**Answer:** No. You do not write or modify software code. The franchise is a commercial sales, business development, and customer relationship enterprise. TMK's central software engineering team in Pune manages 100% of software development, bug fixes, database hosting, and technical deployments. Your primary role is local relationship building, arranging product demonstrations, and driving local sales.

### Q2: What is the FOFO (Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated) model?
**Answer:** FOFO means you own and operate your local commercial office and hire your local sales team, while utilizing TMK's established brand, certified software products, cloud infrastructure, and central billing systems.

### Q3: Why is TMK franchising rather than hiring its own direct sales team?
**Answer:** In India's Tier-2, Tier-3, and rural talukas, institutional customers (such as school trustees and housing societies) place high value on local trust and physical commercial presence. A local entrepreneur with deep roots in the community converts leads significantly faster and provides superior relationship management compared to remote corporate callers.

## 2. Commercial Terms, Fees & Revenue Sharing

### Q4: What is the difference between the ₹25,000 promotional fee and the ₹50,000 regular fee?
**Answer:** The ₹25,000 per product fee is an early-adopter promotional rate offered exclusively to Wave 1 pilot partners. Once local market presence is established, standard franchise fees transition to ₹50,000 per product.

### Q5: Can I buy rights to just one product, like School ERP?
**Answer:** Yes. You may acquire individual product rights (e.g., School ERP for ₹25,000 promotional net fee) or acquire the complete 6-product suite (₹1,50,000 promotional net fee), which grants complete exclusivity across all products in your Taluka.

### Q6: How does the 40:60 revenue sharing work?
**Answer:** On every net software sale and annual subscription renewal generated from your territory, you receive 40% gross commission. TMK retains 60% to fund ongoing software R&D, cloud server costs, customer implementation, and central helpdesk support.

### Q7: Does the 40% commission apply to GST?
**Answer:** No. Indian GST (18%) is a statutory government tax collected from the customer and remitted 100% to the government. Commissions are computed strictly on the Net Revenue amount.

### Q8: When and how are commissions paid?
**Answer:** Commissions are reconciled monthly. A statement is generated by the 5th of each month, and funds are disbursed directly to your commercial bank account via NEFT/RTGS by the 10th.

## 3. Operational Requirements & Office Setup

### Q9: What are the office premises requirements?
**Answer:** A dedicated commercial office of at least 200 sq. ft. with standard business broadband, a computer workstation, and TMK branded interior signage.

### Q10: Am I required to hire a salesperson immediately?
**Answer:** Yes. Contractual covenants mandate at least one dedicated full-time salesperson dedicated exclusively to prospect discovery and demonstration coordination within your territory.

### Q11: Who trains my salesperson?
**Answer:** TMK conducts an intensive 10-day Product Certification Bootcamp covering live demonstrations, pricing proposals, and objection handling. Passing the certification exam is required prior to commercial launch.
