# Infographic Visual Specification: FOFO Operating Model Architecture

## 1. Visual Purpose & Placement
This visual graphic specification is designed for marketing brochures, web portals, and pitch deck Slide 4 to communicate the clean division of operational responsibilities between the Franchise Partner and TMK Central.

---

## 2. Layout Structure & Color Palette
- **Canvas Ratio:** 16:9 Landscape or 1:1 Square Infographic Card.
- **Palette:**
  - Franchisee Domain: Deep Orange (`#E36C0A`) representing local entrepreneurial energy.
  - Franchisor Domain: Navy Blue (`#1F497D`) representing enterprise technical stability.
  - Shared Customer Touchpoint: Teal / Slate (`#2E75B6`).
