# Infographic Visual Specification: Revenue Sharing Waterfall

## 1. Visual Purpose & Design Goal
To visually illustrate how customer payments flow from gross customer invoice to statutory tax remittance, followed by the transparent 40:60 revenue distribution.

---

## 2. Waterfall Step-by-Step Graphic

```text
[ 1. CUSTOMER INVOICED GROSS BILLING ]
      Example: ₹70,800 (School ERP)
                   |
                   v
[ 2. STATUTORY TAX DEDUCTION ]
      Deduct 18% GST (₹10,800) --> Directly remitted to Indian Tax Authorities
                   |
                   v
[ 3. DISTRIBUTABLE NET REALIZED REVENUE ]
      Net Software Amount: ₹60,000 (100% Net Base)
                   |
         +---------+---------+
         |                   |
         v                   v
[ 40% FRANCHISEE SHARE ]    [ 60% FRANCHISOR SHARE ]
      ₹24,000                     ₹36,000
Disbursed directly by 10th   Reinvested in:
• Office Profit Margin       • Software R&D & Features
• Salesperson Commission     • Cloud Hosting & Database
• Local Marketing Fund       • Implementation & L2 Support
```

*Total transparency: Zero hidden deductions. You earn exactly 40% of every net rupee closed in your territory.*
