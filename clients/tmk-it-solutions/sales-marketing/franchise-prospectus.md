# TMK IT Solutions LLP: Institutional Franchise Prospectus

## 1. Corporate Profile & Governance
- **Franchisor Entity:** TMK IT Solutions LLP
- **Headquarters:** Pune, Maharashtra, India
- **Authorized Representative:** Head of Franchise Expansion
- **Legal Form:** Limited Liability Partnership registered under the LLP Act, 2008 (India).

---

## 2. Business Description & Product Portfolio
TMK IT Solutions LLP designs, builds, hosts, and supports commercial enterprise and institutional software products. Under this franchise offering, TMK licenses exclusive territorial marketing and customer relationship management rights to independent commercial franchisees within designated administrative Talukas.

---

## 3. Initial Fees and Recurring Financial Covenants

### 3.1. Upfront Joining Fee
- **Promotional Phase Rate:** ₹25,000 per product (+18% GST). Complete 6-product bundle: ₹1,50,000 (+18% GST).
- **Regular Commercial Rate:** ₹50,000 per product (+18% GST).
- **Refundability:** Non-refundable once operational training commences.

### 3.2. Revenue Sharing & Royalty Covenants
- **Software License Share:** 40% of realized Net Revenue is disbursed to the franchisee. 60% is retained by TMK.
- **Tax Pass-Through:** Indian GST (18%) is excluded from distributable revenue and remitted directly to the government.
- **Settlement Terms:** Commission statements are issued by the 5th of each calendar month; funds disbursed via direct bank transfer by the 10th.

---

## 4. Franchisee Investment & Working Capital Requirement
- **Office Infrastructure (Lease & Setup):** ₹71,000 to ₹90,000.
- **IT Hardware & Office Equipment:** ₹45,000 to ₹55,000.
- **Mandatory 4-Month Solvency Reserve:** Minimum ₹1,40,000 held in liquid bank funds.
- **Total Initial Capital Outlay:** ₹4,16,000 to ₹4,28,000.

---

## 5. Territory & Renewal Terms
- **Initial Contract Term:** 3 Years from date of execution.
- **Renewal Option:** 3 additional years subject to meeting minimum performance quotas and compliance with brand standards.
- **Territory Exclusivity:** Taluka-exclusive during the initial term, protected by central CRM deal registration.

---

## 6. Mandatory Risk Disclosure Statement
> [!WARNING]
> Franchise investments carry commercial risk. Past software sales figures and market demand estimates do not guarantee specific income levels. Operating profitability is dependent upon the franchisee's local sales diligence, salesperson competency, local market demand, and adherence to TMK operational playbooks. Prospective partners are advised to conduct independent due diligence.
