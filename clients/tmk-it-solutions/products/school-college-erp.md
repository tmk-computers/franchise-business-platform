# Product Profile: School / College ERP

## 1. Product Summary & Scope
- **Product ID:** `prd-01-school-college-erp`
- **Product Name:** School / College ERP
- **Category:** Education IT & Campus Automation
- **Delivery Model:** Multi-Tenant SaaS (Cloud Web Portal + Android/iOS Mobile Apps)
- **Status of Pricing Information:** Illustrative / Proposed (₹60,000 / year Net for standard institutional tier)

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments
- K-12 Private English Medium & Regional Medium Schools (500 to 3,000 students).
- Degree Colleges (Arts, Science, Commerce, Engineering, Polytechnic).
- Coaching Institutes and Higher Secondary Junior Colleges.
- Key Decision-Makers: School Trustees, Principals, Administrative Directors, Head Clerks.

---

## 3. Customer Problems & Pain Points
1. **Manual Fee Tracking & Default Risk:** Managing fee receipts via physical paper ledgers leads to reconciliation errors, delayed payments, and uncollected dues.
2. **Attendance Leakage:** Physical roll calls waste 10–15 minutes per period; parents lack real-time visibility into student absenteeism.
3. **Examination & Report Card Friction:** Generating end-of-term report cards, grading calculations, and rank generation takes teachers weeks of manual clerical work.
4. **Fragmented Communication:** Urgent circulars, homework assignments, and emergency closures sent via unmanaged WhatsApp groups lack official records.

---

## 4. Potential Value & Customer Benefits
- **Automated Digital Invoicing & Payment Gateway:** Parents pay school fees directly via UPI, NetBanking, or Credit Card, with automatic instant receipts and auto-reconciliation.
- **Biometric / Mobile Attendance:** Teachers record attendance in 60 seconds; parents receive instant automated SMS/app notifications for absent students.
- **Dynamic Report Card Generation:** One-click generation of CBSE, ICSE, and State Board compliant grade cards.
- **Clerical Overhead Reduction:** Reduces administrative paperwork by up to 60%, freeing faculty to focus on academic instruction.

---

## 5. Sales Approach & Demonstration Strategy
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** 30 to 60 days (Peak purchasing window: January to May ahead of the academic year).
- **Pitch Strategy:** The local franchisee conducts an in-person meeting with the School Principal or Trustee, performing a discovery audit of their fee collection pain points.
- **Live Demonstration:** A scheduled 45-minute live walkthrough led jointly by the franchisee salesperson and TMK central solution architect showcasing fee ledger automation and parent mobile app alerts.

---

## 6. Implementation Considerations
- **Data Migration:** Bulk importing student rosters, parent contact details, and fee structures from legacy Excel sheets.
- **Staff Training:** 3-day remote or on-site training for school administrative clerks and teachers.
- **Go-Live Window:** Typically takes 5 to 10 business days from payment receipt.

---

## 7. Support & Maintenance Considerations
- **Level-1 Support (Franchisee):** Local relationship management, in-person training refreshers, fee receipt template customization requests.
- **Level-2/3 Support (TMK Central):** Cloud database uptime, automated nightly backups, software bug fixes, mobile app updates on Google Play / Apple App Store.

---

## 8. Competitor Benchmarking & Research Placeholders
