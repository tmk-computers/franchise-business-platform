# Product Profile: Sales CRM Platform

## 1. Product Summary & Scope
- **Product ID:** `prd-06-sales-crm`
- **Product Name:** Sales CRM Platform
- **Category:** Commercial IT & Field Sales Enablement
- **Delivery Model:** Cloud SaaS (Field Agent Mobile App + Manager Web Portal)
- **Status of Pricing Information:** Illustrative / Proposed (₹35,000 / year Net for 5-user sales team)

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments
- Real Estate Channel Partners & Property Brokers.
- Automobile Dealerships, Commercial Equipment Distributors.
- Financial Advisory & Insurance Brokerages.
- B2B Distribution & Manufacturing Field Sales Teams.
- Key Decision-Makers: Managing Directors, Sales Heads, Commercial Directors.

---

## 3. Customer Problems & Pain Points
1. **Lead Leakage & Dropped Inquiries:** Leads arriving from Facebook, Google Ads, and websites are not assigned immediately, causing prospects to go cold.
2. **Lack of Field Sales Visibility:** Sales managers cannot verify whether field representatives actually visited client offices or followed up on scheduled meetings.
3. **No Central Customer History:** When a salesperson resigns, prospect call histories, price quotations, and negotiation notes are lost with them.
4. **Manual WhatsApp Messaging:** Sales reps spend hours manually copy-pasting brochures and quotations on personal WhatsApp numbers.

---

## 4. Potential Value & Customer Benefits
- **Omnichannel Lead Aggregation:** Auto-capture leads from Meta Ads, Google, IndiaMART, Justdial, and web landing pages into a single pipeline.
- **Geo-Tagged Field Check-Ins:** Mobile app logs GPS-verified arrival times at client locations during sales meetings.
- **Official WhatsApp Business API Automation:** Send automated product brochures, quotations, and meeting reminders directly through official business WhatsApp channels.
- **Executive Revenue Forecasting:** Real-time visibility into deal stages, closing probabilities, and rep productivity.

---

## 5. Sales Approach & Demonstration Strategy
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** 21 to 45 days.
- **Pitch Strategy:** High-converting product for local distributors, real estate brokers, and B2B trading firms in commercial market centers.
- **Live Demonstration:** Interactive walkthrough demonstrating inbound lead capture on mobile and instant automated WhatsApp greeting dispatch.

---

## 6. Implementation Considerations
- **Lead Pipeline Customization:** Mapping custom sales stages and deal tags.
- **WhatsApp API Verification:** Assisting customer in Meta Business Manager verification.

---

## 7. Support & Maintenance Considerations
- **Level-1 Support (Franchisee):** Sales user onboarding, pipeline reconfiguration, CSV contact imports.
- **Level-2/3 Support (TMK Central):** API integrations, WhatsApp webhook reliability, database scaling.

---

## 8. Competitor Benchmarking & Research Placeholders
- **Primary Incumbents:** LeadSquared, Zoho CRM, TeleCMO, Kylas, SolidPerformers.
- **Documented Competitor Gaps (To be validated by Analyst 1):** High per-user pricing of enterprise CRMs; complex configuration requiring certified consultants.
