# Product Profile: Invoicing App + Web

## 1. Product Summary & Scope
- **Product ID:** `prd-02-invoicing-app-web`
- **Product Name:** Invoicing App + Web
- **Category:** FinTech / MSME Billing & Inventory
- **Delivery Model:** Multi-Platform SaaS (Web Browser Portal + Android Mobile App)
- **Status of Pricing Information:** Illustrative / Proposed (₹12,000 / year Net per business entity)

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments
- Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs), Retailers, Hardware Stores, Electrical Distributors, Wholesalers, Garment Boutiques, Professional Freelancers.
- Key Decision-Makers: Business Proprietors, Managing Partners, Shop Accountants.

---

## 3. Customer Problems & Pain Points
1. **GST Compliance Burden:** Generating GSTR-1, GSTR-2B, and GSTR-3B reports manually from paper bills causes filing delays and statutory penalties.
2. **Inventory Stockout & Pilferage:** Lack of automated inventory tracking leads to stock discrepancies and missed sales.
3. **Delayed Receivable Collections:** Chasing overdue customer payments without automated payment reminder links ties up working capital.
4. **Desktop Lock-In:** Traditional accounting packages (e.g., desktop Tally) cannot be accessed remotely by business owners traveling on the road.

---

## 4. Potential Value & Customer Benefits
- **Multi-Device Cloud Access:** Create professional invoices on mobile or web; data synchronizes in real time.
- **One-Click GST Filing Reports:** Automated tax summaries ready for export to chartered accountants.
- **WhatsApp Invoicing & UPI Payment Links:** Invoices delivered directly to customer WhatsApp with embedded QR codes and instant payment notification.
- **Barcode Scanning & Thermal Printing:** Instant thermal receipt generation for high-speed retail checkout counters.

---

## 5. Sales Approach & Demonstration Strategy
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** 7 to 21 days (High-velocity volume product).
- **Pitch Strategy:** High-density merchant walk-ins in local market yards, wholesale trade clusters, and industrial zones.
- **Live Demonstration:** 15-minute live mobile demonstration showcasing instant invoice creation on an Android smartphone and WhatsApp dispatch.

---

## 6. Implementation Considerations
- **Item Master Upload:** Bulk uploading product catalogs and opening inventory from Excel.
- **Zero Heavy Infrastructure:** Requires only a standard smartphone, tablet, or PC with basic internet connectivity.

---

## 7. Support & Maintenance Considerations
- **Level-1 Support (Franchisee):** Printer pairing, invoice template customization, user password resets.
- **Level-2/3 Support (TMK Central):** Tax rate updates (e.g., GST council changes), cloud backup, payment gateway API integrations.

---

## 8. Competitor Benchmarking & Research Placeholders
- **Primary Incumbents:** Vyapar, myBillBook, Khatabook, Marg ERP, Busy Accounting.
- **Documented Competitor Gaps (To be validated by Analyst 1):** Customer service frustration with pure online chatbots; demand for an on-ground local representative to assist shopkeepers in initial setup.

---
