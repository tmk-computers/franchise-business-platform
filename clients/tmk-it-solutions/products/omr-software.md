# Product Profile: OMR Evaluation Software

## 1. Product Summary & Scope
- **Product ID:** `prd-03-omr-software`
- **Product Name:** OMR Evaluation Software
- **Category:** Assessment IT & Examination Processing
- **Delivery Model:** Desktop Software + Cloud Analytics Sync
- **Status of Pricing Information:** Illustrative / Proposed (₹25,000 / year Net per institutional license)

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments
- Competitive Exam Coaching Institutes (IIT-JEE, NEET, MHT-CET, UPSC, Banking, Defense).
- Private High Schools, Junior Colleges, and University Autonomous Examination Departments.
- Corporate HR Teams conducting large-scale pre-employment recruitment tests.
- Key Decision-Makers: Coaching Institute Directors, Exam Controllers, Academic Heads.

---

## 3. Customer Problems & Pain Points
1. **Slow Manual Test Checking:** Manually grading 500+ objective exam sheets takes faculty days, delaying result declarations.
2. **Expensive Dedicated Hardware Scanners:** Traditional legacy OMR machines require specialized scanning hardware costing ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,000,000.
3. **High Paper Costs:** Proprietary high-gsm pre-printed OMR sheets add significant operating expense per student test.
4. **Human Evaluation Errors:** Tired faculty make grading errors when checking thousands of bubble answer keys.

---

## 4. Potential Value & Customer Benefits
- **Standard Scanner Compatibility:** Runs on standard, low-cost office document scanners (flatbed or ADF sheet-fed scanners).
- **Plain Paper OMR Design:** Print custom bubble answer sheets on standard, inexpensive 70–80 GSM photocopy paper.
- **High-Speed Processing:** Processes up to 100 to 300 answer sheets per minute with 99.9% optical accuracy.
- **Instant Student Performance Analytics:** Instant SMS rank dispatch to students and parents, topic-wise weakness analysis, and cut-off score prediction.

---

## 5. Sales Approach & Demonstration Strategy
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** 14 to 30 days.
- **Pitch Strategy:** Target coaching institute hubs in regional education centers (e.g., Pune, Latur, Kota, Kolhapur, Surat).
- **Live Demonstration:** Bring a batch of 50 test bubble sheets to the client office, scan them on the client's existing office printer, and produce a ranked merit list within 3 minutes.

---

## 6. Implementation Considerations
- **Software Installation:** Simple Windows/Mac installation on institute desktop PCs.
- **Scanner Calibration:** Calibrating optical contrast and bubble coordinate alignment.

---

## 7. Support & Maintenance Considerations
- **Level-1 Support (Franchisee):** Sheet template design, scanner DPI configuration assistance.
- **Level-2/3 Support (TMK Central):** Core image processing engine updates, algorithm enhancements for handling skewed scans.
