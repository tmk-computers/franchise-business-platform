# Product Profile: Property Management ERP

## 1. Product Summary & Scope
- **Product ID:** `prd-04-property-management-erp`
- **Product Name:** Property Management ERP
- **Category:** Real Estate IT & Housing Society Management
- **Delivery Model:** Cloud SaaS (Resident Mobile App + Committee Admin Web Dashboard + Guard Tablet App)
- **Status of Pricing Information:** Illustrative / Proposed (₹75,000 / year Net for standard residential complex)

---

## 2. Target Customer Segments
- Co-operative Housing Societies (CHS), Residential Welfare Associations (RWA), Gated Villa Communities (50 to 1,000+ units).
- Commercial Business Parks and Shopping Complexes.
- Facility Management Companies.
- Key Decision-Makers: Society Management Committee Chairmen, Secretaries, Treasurers, Estate Managers.

---

## 3. Customer Problems & Pain Points
1. **Maintenance Dues Default:** Tracking unpaid monthly society dues, penalty interest, and issuing paper bills is time-consuming and fraught with resident disputes.
2. **Society Accounting Audits:** Maintaining cash books, vendor balance sheets, and audit ledgers requires costly external accounting services.
3. **Security Gate Lapses:** Manual visitor paper registers at security entry gates cannot verify visitor identity or prevent unauthorized entry.
4. **Resident Helpdesk Inefficiency:** Complaints regarding plumbing, elevators, or electrical faults logged via paper books get lost without resolution tracking.

---

## 4. Potential Value & Customer Benefits
- **Automated Society Billing & Collection:** Automated monthly maintenance bill generation with integrated UPI/card payment links; dues auto-reconcile with bank accounts.
- **Full Society Accounting Suite:** Compliant balance sheets, income-expenditure statements, and audit-ready society ledgers.
- **Digital Gate Security App:** Tablet-based visitor photo capture, vehicle license plate logging, and resident app pre-approvals for guests and delivery agents.
- **Digital Ticketing & Facility Booking:** Residents book clubhouses or raise maintenance tickets directly from their smartphone app.

---

## 5. Sales Approach & Demonstration Strategy
- **Sales Cycle Duration:** 45 to 90 days (Requires Society Managing Committee consensus or AGM approval).
- **Pitch Strategy:** Target newly formed residential townships and established societies facing accounting bottlenecks.
- **Live Demonstration:** Evening presentation delivered to the Society Managing Committee demonstrating automated billing runs and security gate verification.

---

## 6. Implementation Considerations
- **Resident Master Data Onboarding:** Uploading flat numbers, owner vs. tenant data, vehicle details.
- **Opening Dues Setup:** Transferring past balance arrears into the system ledger.
- **Guard Training:** 1-day on-site training for security guard staff.

---

## 7. Support & Maintenance Considerations
- **Level-1 Support (Franchisee):** Committee handover training, billing disputes, guard tablet replacement.
- **Level-2/3 Support (TMK Central):** Cloud server scaling, bank gateway reconciliation, resident mobile app updates.

---

## 8. Competitor Benchmarking & Research Placeholders
- **Primary Incumbents:** MyGate, NoBrokerHood, ApnaComplex, ADDA.
- **Documented Competitor Gaps (To be validated by Analyst 1):** Competitors often monetize resident data with aggressive third-party advertising; demand for privacy-first, ad-free society software.
