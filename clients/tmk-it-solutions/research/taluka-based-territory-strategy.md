# Research Brief: Taluka-Based Territory Demarcation & Rollout Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
- **Lead Investigator:** Analyst 1
- **Reviewer:** Analyst 2
- **Objective:** Establish the geospatial and demographic justification for adopting the **Taluka / Tehsil** as the fundamental territory allocation unit for TMK IT Solutions in India.

---

## 2. Geospatial Justification for Taluka-Level Allocation
1. **Administrative Clarity:** India comprises over 6,000 sub-districts (Talukas / Tehsils). Each taluka has clearly demarcated government boundaries, eliminating disputes over ambiguous radial distance boundaries (e.g., "within 10 km of office").
2. **Manageable Field Travel Span:** A typical taluka spans a geographic radius of 15 to 30 km, enabling a single dedicated salesperson to travel on a two-wheeler to any school or client site within 45 minutes.
3. **Adequate Target Institution Density:** A typical Tier-2 or Tier-3 taluka contains between 300 and 1,200 commercial establishments, schools, and housing societies, comfortably exceeding the Minimum Sustainable Market Sizing ($MSMS$) threshold of 600 qualified institutions.

---

## 3. Wave 1 Priority Talukas (Western Maharashtra Pilot)
- **Haveli Taluka (Pune District):** 1,200+ target entities; massive education and housing growth.
- **Baramati Taluka (Pune District):** 550+ institutions; major educational and cooperative agro-business hub.
- **Satara Taluka (Satara District):** 420+ schools and coaching centers.
- **Nashik Taluka (Nashik District):** 900+ commercial and education targets.
- **Kolhapur Karvir Taluka (Kolhapur District):** 750+ institutions.
