# Research Brief: Six-Product Market Demand Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
- **Lead Investigator:** Analyst 1
- **Reviewer:** Analyst 2
- **Objective:** Evaluate addressable customer demand, buying triggers, and seasonality across the six TMK software products.

---

## 2. Product-by-Product Demand Profile

### 2.1. School / College ERP
- **Demand Trigger:** Urgent need for automated fee reconciliation, biometric attendance, and board-compliant report cards.
- **Seasonality:** Highly seasonal (February to June).
- **Target Density:** 150 to 450 private schools per standard Indian district.
- **Willingness to Pay:** Moderate to High (₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000 / year depending on student strength).

### 2.2. Invoicing App + Web
- **Demand Trigger:** Mandatory electronic invoicing (e-Invoice) and quarterly GST filing audits.
- **Seasonality:** Steady monthly demand with surges around financial year-end (March–April).
- **Target Density:** 5,000 to 20,000 retail/wholesale MSME shops per district.
- **Willingness to Pay:** Elastic (₹6,000 to ₹15,000 / year).

### 2.3. OMR Software
- **Demand Trigger:** Coaching institutes conducting weekly mock tests for NEET/JEE entrance exams.
- **Seasonality:** August to January during intensive test series preparation.
- **Target Density:** 40 to 120 coaching academies per commercial district.
- **Willingness to Pay:** High (instant replacement for manual grading labor).

### 2.4. Property Management ERP
- **Demand Trigger:** Disputes over maintenance defaults, tenant verification, and society accounting audits.
- **Seasonality:** Steady throughout the year.
- **Target Density:** 100 to 500 registered housing societies in urbanizing talukas.
- **Willingness to Pay:** High (billed to society maintenance reserve).

### 2.5. DevOps Automation Platform
- **Demand Trigger:** Engineering agencies seeking to reduce cloud infrastructure hosting bills and deployment errors.
- **Seasonality:** Neutral.
- **Target Density:** Focused on IT corridors (Pune, Ahmedabad, Nashik).
- **Willingness to Pay:** High (direct technical ROI).

### 2.6. Sales CRM Platform
- **Demand Trigger:** High field sales turnover, lost WhatsApp leads, lack of salesperson GPS tracking.
- **Seasonality:** Steady.
- **Target Density:** Real estate channel partners, auto dealers, manufacturing distributors.
- **Willingness to Pay:** Moderate (₹25,000 to ₹50,000 / year).
