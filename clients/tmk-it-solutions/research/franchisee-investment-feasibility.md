# Research Brief: Franchisee Investment Feasibility & Working Capital Analysis

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
- **Lead Investigator:** Analyst 2 (Finance, Analytics & Intelligence)
- **Reviewer:** Analyst 1 (Strategy, Research & Documentation)
- **Objective:** Quantify the financial viability, maximum drawdown, and capital recovery trajectory for a prospective franchise partner investing ₹3.26 Lakhs to ₹4.28 Lakhs.

---

## 2. Quantitative Solvency & Cash Runway Findings

### 2.1. Initial Capital Breakdown
- **Franchise Joining Fee:** ₹1,50,000 (Promotional Net for all 6 products).
- **Physical Office Setup:** ₹1,26,000 (Rental deposit, signage, IT hardware, workstations).
- **Mandatory Working Capital Reserve:** ₹1,40,000 (4 months of fixed overhead).
- **Total Initial Capital Required:** ₹4,16,000 to ₹4,28,000.

### 2.2. Unit Break-Even & Payback Analysis
- **Fixed Monthly OpEx:** Rent (₹12,000) + Salesperson (₹18,000) + Utilities & Travel (₹6,500) = ₹36,500 / month.
- **Blended Commission Yield per Sale (40%):** ~₹18,800 net commission per blended product sale.
- **Monthly Break-Even Sales:** $\frac{₹36,500}{₹18,800} \approx 1.94 \text{ software sales / month}$.
- **Base Case Payback Horizon:** Month 11 to Month 19 depending on initial salesperson ramp-up velocity.

---

## 3. Financial Viability Conclusion
The unit economics demonstrate robust financial feasibility provided the franchisee maintains the required 4-month working capital reserve. Closing 3 software deals per month generates ₹56,400 in gross commission, producing an immediate net operating profit of ₹19,900/month in Year 1, rising to over ₹45,000/month in Year 2 as recurring renewals compound.
