# Research Brief: Competitor Landscape & Pricing Benchmarks

## 1. Executive Summary & Objective
- **Lead Investigator:** Analyst 1
- **Reviewer:** Analyst 2
- **Objective:** Map direct competitors across each product vertical, identifying documented pricing models, distribution channels, and feature differentiation.

---

## 2. Competitive Benchmarking Matrix

| Product Vertical | Key Incumbent Competitors | Incumbent Pricing Model | Incumbent Distribution | Documented Gaps & TMK Opportunities |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **School ERP** | Teachmint, Fedena, Vidyalaya, Entab | ₹10 to ₹30 per student / month, or ₹50k - ₹1.5L annual license | Direct digital inside sales / EdTech sales reps | Lack of localized vernacular support; rigid contract terms; expensive customization. |
| **Invoicing App** | Vyapar, myBillBook, Khatabook, Marg ERP | ₹2,500 to ₹10,000 / year (desktop/mobile subscription) | Digital marketing, app store downloads, IT hardware dealers | Pure digital chatbots provide poor support; non-technical shopkeepers need on-site setup help. |
| **OMR Software** | Addmen, Multigraphics, Think Exam | ₹30,000 to ₹80,000 perpetual license + annual AMC | Direct regional agents | Complex legacy UI; high annual maintenance costs. |
| **Property ERP** | MyGate, NoBrokerHood, ADDA, ApnaComplex | ₹20 to ₹50 per flat / month, or free ad-supported tier | B2B direct field sales in metro cities | Aggressive resident data monetization; ad bombardment; poor focus on Tier-2/3 towns. |
| **DevOps Platform**| GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, AWS CodePipeline | $20 to $100 per user / month (USD billing) | Self-service cloud SaaS | High US-dollar pricing; lack of Indian GST invoices; requires deep cloud expertise. |
| **Sales CRM** | LeadSquared, Zoho CRM, Kylas, TeleCMO | ₹1,000 to ₹3,500 per user / month | Digital inbound marketing / enterprise sales | Expensive per-user seat penalties; complex configuration workflows. |

---

## 3. Pricing Strategy Recommendation
- **Transparent Flat Annual Fee:** Avoid charging predatory per-student or per-flat fees that penalize growing institutions. A predictable flat annual fee with 40% local franchisee retention creates high sales velocity and trusted relationships.
