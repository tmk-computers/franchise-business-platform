- **Lead Investigator:** Analyst 1 (Strategy, Research & Documentation)
- **Reviewer:** Analyst 2 (Finance, Analytics & Intelligence)
- **Topic:** Evolution, market dynamics, and viability of distributed franchise models for B2B software in India.
- **Evidence Cross-Reference:** Logged in `analyst-workspace/research-methodology/research-evidence-register.md`.

---

## 2. Market Context & Macro Drivers
1. **The Digitization Wave in Tier-2/3 India:** Digital payments (UPI), GST compliance mandates, and the national NEP 2020 education policy have catalyzed software adoption among schools, traders, and small enterprises across semi-urban India.
2. **Failure of Pure-Play Remote Inside Sales:** While SaaS enterprises (e.g., Salesforce, Freshworks) succeed with remote inside sales in metro tech corridors, local institutions in non-metro talukas strongly prefer in-person product demonstrations, physical commercial presence, and vernacular assistance.
3. **The Channel Distribution Opportunity:** Franchising bridges this trust divide. An established software franchisor provides reliable multi-tenant software engineering, while local entrepreneurs leverage existing community relationships to accelerate customer acquisition.

---

## 3. Working Hypotheses to be Empirically Validated
- **Hypothesis H-01:** Local institutional buyers (schools, housing societies) convert at 3x higher rates when pitched by a resident local franchise owner versus an unknown corporate caller from Bangalore or Mumbai.
- **Hypothesis H-02:** A portfolio of 6 diverse products reduces franchisee revenue cyclicality, allowing steady cash flow across different purchasing seasons (e.g., School ERP peak in April–May; Invoicing App year-round).
