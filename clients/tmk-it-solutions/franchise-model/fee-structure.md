# TMK Franchise Fee Structure & Pricing Policy

## 1. Executive Summary of Fee Schedule

| Fee Component | Promotional Launch Tier (Net) | Regular Commercial Tier (Net) | Applicable Taxes | Payment Terms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Per-Product Franchise Joining Fee** | **₹25,000** per product | **₹50,000** per product | +18% GST | 100% upfront upon contract signing |
| **Complete Portfolio Bundle (All 6 Products)** | **₹1,50,000** bundle | **₹3,00,000** bundle | +18% GST | 100% upfront upon contract signing |
| **Annual Franchise Agreement Renewal Fee** | Nil / ₹10,000 administrative | ₹25,000 per territory | +18% GST | At end of 3-year initial term |

---

## 2. Policy Provisions & Refundability Terms

1. **Non-Refundable Status:**
   The joining fee grants the franchisee exclusive commercial territory representation rights and covers initial onboarding, training, and sales enablement collateral. Joining fees are non-refundable once the 14-day training bootcamp commences.
2. **Promotional Eligibility Window:**
   The promotional fee of ₹25,000 per product is restricted to Wave 1 early-adopter partners committing to open their commercial office within 30 days of agreement execution.
3. **Product Selection Flexibility:**
   Franchisees may elect to onboard either:
   - Specific targeted products (e.g., School ERP + OMR Software = ₹50,000 promotional net fee).
   - The full enterprise suite of 6 products (₹1,50,000 promotional net fee), which confers exclusivity across all software domains within the assigned Taluka.
