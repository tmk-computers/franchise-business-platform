# TMK Territory Allocation & Exclusivity Policy Draft

## 1. Territory Definition
Territories are allocated strictly according to official Indian Government administrative boundaries: **Taluka / Tehsil** in semi-urban and rural areas, or **Municipal Ward Clusters** in metropolitan cities.

---

## 2. Exclusivity Covenants
1. **Exclusive Sales Representation:** TMK IT Solutions LLP shall not appoint another franchise partner for the contracted software products within the designated Taluka during the term of the agreement, provided performance milestones are maintained.
2. **Deal Registration & Lead Locking:** When a franchisee registers an enterprise prospect in the central CRM with verified discovery meeting notes, that account is locked exclusively to the franchisee for 90 days. No other franchisee or direct TMK sales representative may pitch that specific institution during the lock period.
3. **Territory Minimum Quota:** To preserve exclusivity, the franchisee must close a minimum of:
   - 3 software sales in Quarter 1 (ramp-up).
   - 6 software sales per quarter from Quarter 2 onward.
   Failure to meet the quota for two consecutive quarters allows TMK to convert the territory to Non-Exclusive status after a 30-day cure period.
