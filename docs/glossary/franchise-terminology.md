# Glossary of Franchise & Commercial Terms

## 1. Franchise Model Terms
- **FOFO (Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated):** The franchisee funds capital, leases the office, and manages operations; franchisor provides branding, software, training, and centralized delivery.
- **FOCO (Franchise Owned, Company Operated):** The franchisee funds capital; franchisor operates the unit directly, sharing net margins.
- **COCO (Company Owned, Company Operated):** 100% corporate flagship unit owned and operated by the franchisor.
- **Master Franchise:** Exclusive regional developer with rights to sub-franchise within a major state or country.

## 2. Financial Terms
- **Net Realized Revenue:** Gross customer billing minus statutory taxes (e.g., 18% GST).
- **Revenue Sharing Ratio:** Contractual split of net revenue between franchisee and franchisor (e.g., 40:60).
- **CapEx (Capital Expenditure):** Upfront setup outlays including office rental deposit, interior signage, and IT hardware.
- **Working Capital Reserve:** Mandatory cash cushion covering 4 to 6 months of fixed operating overhead.
- **Break-Even Unit Sales:** Minimum number of software contracts required per month to clear all fixed operating costs.
- **Payback Period:** Number of operating months required for cumulative operating cash flow to fully recover initial investment.

## 3. Geospatial & Governance Terms
- **Taluka / Tehsil:** Official Indian sub-district administrative boundary used as the fundamental territory unit.
- **Deal Registration Lock:** 90-day exclusive hold granted to a franchisee when an enterprise prospect is logged in the central CRM.
