## Learning Outcomes

Upon completing this section, an analyst will be able to:
1. **Compare Franchise Archetypes:** Clearly contrast capital requirements, operational responsibilities, and margin profiles across FOFO, FOCO, COCO, and Master Franchises.
2. **Formulate RACI Matrices:** Define precise boundary demarcations between franchisor duties (central software engineering, brand marketing, billing infrastructure) and franchisee duties (local sales, customer demos, first-line support).
3. **Model Revenue Distributions:** Structure Gross Billing vs. Net Realized Revenue calculations with mandatory pass-through statutory tax isolation (e.g., 18% GST).
4. **Define Territory Governance:** Establish clear taluka/district territorial boundaries, deal registration lock duration, and benchmark quotas to prevent partner disputes.
5. **Enforce Ethical Disclosure:** Guarantee that financial marketing avoids forbidden terms such as "guaranteed return," "risk-free income," or unsubstantiated profitability claims.

Using [frameworks/franchise-model-design/franchise-archetypes-comparison.md](file:///d:/company/products/franchise-business-platform/frameworks/franchise-model-design/franchise-archetypes-comparison.md), create a comparison matrix contrasting FOFO and FOCO across 4 dimensions: (1) Setup CapEx Burden, (2) Local Staffing Overhead, (3) Franchisor Quality Control Risk, and (4) Franchisee Margin Retention.

### Exercise 2.2: Pass-Through Tax Segregation Calculation
Given a software license sale of ₹1,18,000 (tax inclusive at 18% GST) with a 40:60 revenue sharing agreement:
1. Calculate Net Realized Revenue.
2. Calculate the statutory GST amount.
3. Calculate the Franchisee's 40% distribution.
4. Calculate the Franchisor's 60% distribution.
*(Verify your answers match: Net Revenue = ₹1,00,000; GST = ₹18,000; Franchisee = ₹40,000; Franchisor = ₹60,000)*.
