# Software & IT Services Franchise Economics & Operating Model

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Software and IT services franchising represents an asset-light, high-margin, recurring-revenue model. Unlike traditional brick-and-mortar franchising (e.g., QSR or retail), a software franchise does not involve inventory spoilage, complex physical supply chains, or heavy real estate leases. Instead, the model monetizes intellectual property, localized relationship selling, and distributed implementation services.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **FOFO Hybrid (Recommended):** The franchisee owns a local commercial sales and support office, hiring local salespeople to acquire enterprise, institutional, or SME customers. The franchisor centralizes core software engineering, cloud infrastructure hosting, product demonstrations, and Level-2/Level-3 technical support.
- **Sales Franchise / Value-Added Channel Franchise:** Franchisee operates exclusively as an authorized local sales agency, earning commissions on closed contracts and renewal streams with zero infrastructure ownership.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks & Unit Economics
- **Gross Margins:** 75% to 88% on software license fees (cloud hosting typically accounts for 5% to 12% of revenue).
- **Revenue Sharing Norms:** 30% to 50% retained by the franchisee for sales and local account management; 50% to 70% retained by the franchisor for software R&D, central hosting, and implementation.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback:** 4 to 8 months.
- **Annual Subscription Renewals:** 80% to 92% benchmark for mission-critical ERPs and accounting tools; 70% to 80% for horizontal tools.

---

## 4. Key Success Factors & Operational Boundaries
1. **Separation of Sales vs. Engineering:** Franchisees must focus 100% on pipeline generation, local networking, in-person discovery meetings, and relationship maintenance. They must not attempt to modify source code.
2. **Centralized Billing & Invoicing:** All customer contracts and tax invoices must be issued centrally by the franchisor to ensure GST compliance and maintain single-point license governance.
3. **Mandatory Demo Support:** Franchisor solution architects must be available to co-lead live product demonstrations for complex enterprise deals.
