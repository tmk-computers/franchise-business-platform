## 1. Domain Purpose & Scope
The `industry-models/` directory provides vertical-specific economic benchmarks, margin structures, operational archetypes, and industry risk taxonomies across primary franchise sectors:
- Software & IT Services
- Food & Beverage (QSR, Cafes)
- Retail
- Education & Training
- Healthcare & Diagnostics
- Real Estate Brokerage
- Manufacturing & Fabrication
- Professional Services

## 2. Specialized Agent Rules for `industry-models/`

1. **Empirically Grounded Benchmarks:**
   - Financial benchmarks (COGS %, prime costs, EBITDA margins, payback horizons, royalty rates) must represent validated industry norms.
   - Do NOT invent artificial benchmarks. If industry data is preliminary, clearly label it as `[BENCHMARKED]` or `[ESTIMATED]`.
2. **Industry Neutrality:**
   - Industry profiles describe generalized sector economics, not specific corporate clients.
   - For example, `industry-models/software/` covers B2B software and SaaS franchise economics generally, whereas TMK IT Solutions LLP's specific workspace is in `clients/tmk-it-solutions/`.
3. **Comparative Structure:**
   - Each industry model document must detail:
     1. Industry Archetype Overview
     2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes (FOFO, FOCO, Master, etc.)
     3. Financial Benchmarks & Unit Economics
     4. Critical Operational & Regulatory Risks
