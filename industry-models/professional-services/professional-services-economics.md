# Professional Services Franchise Economics

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Professional services franchising includes recruitment and staffing agencies, accounting and tax practices, corporate compliance consultancies, digital marketing agencies, and executive coaching networks. The business revolves around expert knowledge, standardized methodologies, client retainers, and talent placement.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **FOFO Knowledge Franchise:** Franchisee operates a local office utilizing franchisor proprietary screening algorithms, assessment frameworks, central billing, and international candidate databases.
- **Agency Network Franchise:** Shared backend fulfillment (e.g., SEO/software development performed centrally) with local business development led by franchisees.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 40% to 65%.
- **Revenue Sharing:** 35% to 50% retained by local business developer; 50% to 65% for central delivery and platform.
- **Payback Period:** 6 to 12 months.
