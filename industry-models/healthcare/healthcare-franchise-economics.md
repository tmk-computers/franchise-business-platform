# Healthcare & Diagnostic Franchise Economics & Operating Model

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Healthcare franchising encompasses pathology collection centers, diagnostic imaging hubs, dental clinics, dialysis centers, and retail pharmacy chains. The sector is subject to stringent regulatory oversight, licensing, medical ethics, and quality control.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **Hub-and-Spoke Diagnostic Model:** Franchisees operate collection centers (spokes) collecting patient samples; franchisor operates centralized reference laboratories (hub) performing complex testing.
- **FOCO / FOFO Dental and Eye Care:** Standardized clinical workflows with central supply of equipment and consumables.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **Gross Margin:** 50% to 70% on testing services.
- **Collection Center Commission:** 25% to 40% retained by local center franchisee; 60% to 75% for central testing and pathology review.
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Pre-requisite clinical establishment registrations, bio-medical waste handling contracts.
