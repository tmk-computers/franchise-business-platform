# Real Estate Brokerage & Property Management Franchise Economics

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Real estate franchising (e.g., RE/MAX, Century 21 model) scales residential and commercial brokerage networks, tenancy management, and land advisory services. It relies on local broker networks, agent recruitment, listing syndication, and brand trust.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **Agent Brokerage Franchise:** Franchisee operates a local brokerage office, recruiting licensed independent real estate agents. Franchisee collects agent desk fees and commission splits (e.g., 70/30 agent/broker), paying a 6% to 10% royalty to the franchisor for brand, CRM, and national listing distribution.
- **Property Management Franchise:** Recurring fee model managing rental collections, tenant verification, and maintenance for absent property owners.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **Commission Rates:** 1% to 2% on transaction values; 1 month rent on leasing transactions.
- **Franchise Royalty:** 5% to 8% of gross commission income (GCI).
- **Payback Period:** 12 to 24 months.
