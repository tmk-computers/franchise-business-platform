# Retail Franchise Economics & Operating Model

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Retail franchising spans apparel, consumer electronics, footwear, jewelry, and specialty goods. Profitability hinges on inventory turns, merchandise planning, store location footfall, and working capital financing.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **FOFO (SOR - Sale or Return / Outright Purchase):** Franchisee purchases inventory wholesale or holds on consignment, operating the retail store.
- **FOCO (Company Managed):** Franchisee provides retail high-street real estate; corporate visual merchandisers and store managers run daily operations.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **Gross Margin Spread:** 30% to 45% on retail sales.
- **Store Setup CapEx:** High ($1,500 - $3,500 per sq ft for premium fit-outs).
- **Inventory Holding Days:** 45 to 90 days.
- **EBITDA Margin:** 10% to 16%.
