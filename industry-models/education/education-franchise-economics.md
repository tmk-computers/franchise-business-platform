# Education & Training Franchise Economics & Operating Model

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Education franchising covers K-12 private schools, preschools, test preparation institutes (IIT-JEE, NEET, Civil Services), vocational academies, and coding bootcamps. Revenue is driven by student enrollments with cyclical annual or semi-annual fee collection seasons.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **FOFO:** Highly prevalent in preschools, tutoring centers, and vocational training institutes. The franchisee leases property, hires teachers, and conducts local marketing; the franchisor provides curriculum, study materials, examination systems, and teacher training.
- **Master Franchise / Regional Developer:** Used for statewide expansion of branded preschool chains.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **Royalty Rate:** 12% to 20% of student fee collections, or per-student curriculum material fees.
- **Gross Profit Margin:** 45% to 65%.
- **Faculty & Staff Cost:** 25% to 35% of revenue.
- **Franchisee EBITDA:** 20% to 35%.
- **Seasonality Risk:** Heavy cash inflow during admission months (April–July in India); dry cash flow periods requiring disciplined working capital reserves.
