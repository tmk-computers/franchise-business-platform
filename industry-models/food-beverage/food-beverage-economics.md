# Food & Beverage (F&B) Franchise Economics & Operating Model

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
F&B franchising (Quick Service Restaurants, cafes, cloud kitchens, casual dining) is the most historically mature franchise vertical. It is characterized by high physical capital intensity, strict supply chain dependencies, real estate sensitivity, and tight operational margins.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **FOCO (Franchise Owned, Company Operated):** Common in premium QSR brands where maintaining recipe consistency and customer service standards requires direct corporate operating teams, while real estate and fit-out capital is provided by investor-franchisees.
- **FOFO (Franchise Owned, Franchise Operated):** Common in standardized kiosk and beverage brands with rigid ingredient pre-mixes and automated equipment.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks & Unit Economics
- **Cost of Goods Sold (COGS):** 28% to 35% of gross revenue (food ingredients, packaging).
- **Labor Overhead:** 15% to 22% of gross revenue.
- **Prime Cost (COGS + Labor):** Target $\le 55\%$ for sustainable profitability.
- **Occupancy / Rent:** 8% to 15% of gross revenue.
- **Royalty & Marketing Levies:** 4% to 8% royalty fee plus 1% to 3% national marketing fund fee.
- **Franchisee EBITDA Margin:** 12% to 20%.
- **Capital Payback Period:** 18 to 36 months.

---

## 4. Critical Operational Risks
1. **Supply Chain Leakage:** Franchisees purchasing unapproved local ingredients to cut costs, degrading food taste and violating health standards.
2. **Real Estate Footfall Failure:** Inaccurate site selection resulting in inadequate daily customer volume to clear high fixed retail rents.
