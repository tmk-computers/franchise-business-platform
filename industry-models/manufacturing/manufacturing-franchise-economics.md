# Manufacturing & Fabrication Franchise Economics

## 1. Industry Archetype Overview
Manufacturing franchising (e.g., Coca-Cola bottling model, prefabricated building components, modular furniture, specialty chemicals) delegates local production and localized fulfillment to regional franchise plants while centralizing product formulation, brand marketing, and quality standards.

---

## 2. Dominant Franchise Archetypes
- **Territorial Bottling / Fabrication Franchise:** Regional partner invests in capital plant and machinery, purchasing proprietary concentrate or raw materials exclusively from the franchisor and distributing to local retail/wholesale channels within a protected geographic territory.

---

## 3. Financial Benchmarks
- **CapEx Outlay:** Very high ($250,000 to $2M+).
- **Gross Margin:** 35% to 50%.
- **Working Capital Need:** Extensive (60–90 days inventory plus receivables).
- **Payback Period:** 3 to 5 years.
