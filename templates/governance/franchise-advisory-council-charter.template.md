# Franchise Advisory Council (FAC) Governance Charter

**Template ID:** `TMP-GOV-001`  
**Purpose:** Reusable governance framework establishing a collaborative consultative body composed of elected franchisee representatives and franchisor executive leadership to review product roadmaps, marketing strategies, territory policies, and network health.

---

## 1. Council Mission & Core Objectives
The **Franchise Advisory Council (FAC)** serves as a formal, structured communication channel between the franchisee network and franchisor executive leadership of `{{CLIENT_NAME}}`.

The council is established to:
1. Provide constructive partner feedback on product performance, client requirements, and competitive market developments.
2. Review proposed changes to commercial policies, operations manuals, and territory rules prior to network-wide ratification.
3. Foster collaborative innovation in marketing campaigns and sales enablement materials.
4. Facilitate dispute mitigation and promote overall network cohesion and profitability.

---

## 2. Council Composition & Representation
- **Franchisor Representatives (3 permanent seats):**
  - Managing Partner / CEO (`{{FRANCHISOR_LEAD_ROLE}}`)
  - Head of Product & Engineering (`{{PRODUCT_LEAD_ROLE}}`)
  - Director of Franchise Operations (`{{OPS_LEAD_ROLE}}`)
- **Franchisee Representatives (4 elected regional seats):**
  - Western Region Representative (Elected by active regional franchisees)
  - Northern Region Representative (Elected by active regional franchisees)
  - Southern Region Representative (Elected by active regional franchisees)
  - Eastern / Central Region Representative (Elected by active regional franchisees)

---

## 3. Eligibility & Election Procedures
1. To qualify for election to the FAC, a franchisee must:
   - Have operated an active franchise unit for at least 12 consecutive months.
   - Be in full compliance with all operational, legal, and financial obligations (zero active defaults or cure notices).
   - Meet or exceed minimum quarterly sales benchmark targets.
2. Council terms are for **one calendar year**, with a maximum of two consecutive terms.
3. Elections are held annually in Q4 via confidential digital ballot administered by the Operations Lead.

---

## 4. Operational Rhythm & Meeting Cadence
- **Quarterly Regular Sessions:** Scheduled on the third Thursday of the month concluding each financial quarter.
- **Special Urgent Sessions:** May be convened with 7 days written notice by joint petition of at least 3 franchisee representatives or the Managing Partner.
- **Agenda Protocol:** Agenda items must be submitted 14 days prior to the meeting. Formal minutes are published to all network partners within 7 days post-session.

---

## 5. Non-Binding Advisory Status & Scope
The FAC is an advisory and consultative body. It possesses no legal authority to alter binding contracts, unilaterally set pricing, or bind the franchisor to financial obligations. However, franchisor leadership formally commits to providing written responses to all formal FAC policy recommendations within 30 days.
