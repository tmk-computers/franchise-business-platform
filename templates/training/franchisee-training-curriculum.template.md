# Modular Franchisee Onboarding & Certification Curriculum

**Template ID:** `TMP-TRN-001`  
**Purpose:** Reusable, 10-day intensive onboarding training syllabus for newly inducted franchisees, combining commercial sales training, product software demonstration mastery, operations manual SOPs, and formal accreditation.

---

## 1. Curriculum Overview
- **Franchise System:** `{{CLIENT_NAME}}`
- **Target Audience:** Franchise Operating Principals, Local Sales Managers, and Customer Support Leads.
- **Duration:** 10 Business Days (80 Training Hours).
- **Delivery Mode:** Blended (5 Days Central In-Person / Virtual Intensive + 5 Days Territory Field Practicum).
- **Passing Standard:** Minimum 80% on written theory examination + successful mock product sales demonstration.

---

## 2. Daily Modular Schedule

### Week 1: Foundation, Products & Commercial Sales Mastery

#### Day 1: Brand Vision, Franchise Architecture & Ethics
- Corporate history, leadership values, and product roadmap.
- Franchise model rights, obligations, and the RACI governance matrix.
- Anti-misrepresentation rules and ethical sales guidelines (zero deceptive earnings claims).

#### Day 2–3: Product Portfolio Deep-Dive & Live Navigation
- Core software modules, feature walkthroughs, and architecture.
- Live system navigation using dedicated partner sandbox instances.
- Understanding customer pain points across key commercial verticals.

#### Day 4: Commercial Pitching, Pricing & Demo Scripts
- Standard 5-step demonstration script and prospect qualification.
- Price sheet explanation, implementation fees, and recurring annual renewals.
- Objection handling battlecards (pricing objections, data migration, competitor comparisons).

#### Day 5: CRM Operations & Territory Deal Registration
- Central partner portal training and user management.
- Deal registration SOP: logging prospects, 90-day protection lock rules.
- Quote generation, contract issuance, and invoice workflow.

---

### Week 2: Operations, Central Support & Field Practicum

#### Day 6: Unit Launch, Office Setup & Compliance SOPs
- Office workstation, networking, and security compliance.
- Brand signage, marketing collateral, and official stationery guidelines.
- Mandatory statutory registers and record-keeping requirements.

#### Day 7: Customer Onboarding, Support SLAs & Escalations
- End-client implementation workflow and training best practices.
- Tier-1 local support vs. Tier-2/3 central engineering escalation matrix.
- Ticketing system operations, response SLAs, and customer satisfaction tracking.

#### Day 8: Financial Management, Billing & Tax Segregation
- Net Realized Revenue calculation and 18% GST pass-through tax isolation.
- Commission reconciliation schedules, TDS certificates, and banking escrow.
- Franchisee unit bookkeeping, OpEx management, and cash flow forecasting.

#### Day 9: Live Role-Play & Mock Client Demonstration
- Simulated enterprise client presentation before certification panel.
- Live objection handling and pricing defense.
- Panel feedback and corrective coaching.

#### Day 10: Formal Certification Assessment & Graduation
- Comprehensive written examination (100 multiple choice & short-answer questions).
- Final demo evaluation sign-off.
- Issuance of **Certified Franchise Operator Accreditation** and welcome package dispatch.
