# Master Franchise Pitch Deck Template (10-Slide Specification)

> **Template Usage Instructions:**  
> Use this structure to generate commercial franchise pitch presentations.

---

- **Slide 1: Title Slide**
  - Brand Name, Franchise Opportunity Title, Territory Scope, Confidentiality Notice.
- **Slide 2: Market Problem & Digital Transformation**
  - Local institutional pain points, macro tailwinds, why traditional sales channels fail.
- **Slide 3: Products & Solution Suite**
  - 4 to 6 core commercial products with price points and target customer profiles.
- **Slide 4: Franchise Operating Model (RACI)**
  - Clean separation of franchisee responsibilities vs. central franchisor delivery.
- **Slide 5: Commercial Revenue Sharing**
  - Explicit split percentages, cash flow example, and low break-even unit sales hurdle.
- **Slide 6: Capital Outlay & Investment Breakdown**
  - Joining fee, office infrastructure, IT hardware, and mandatory working capital reserve.
- **Slide 7: Territory Allocation & Exclusivity**
  - Administrative boundary rules, deal registration locking, anti-poaching policies.
- **Slide 8: 30-Day Onboarding & Training Bootcamp**
  - Day-by-day roadmap from contract execution to certified commercial launch.
- **Slide 9: Three-Year Network Expansion Vision**
  - Network scaling targets, unit rollout milestones, compounding renewal cash flows.
- **Slide 10: Call to Action & Contact Information**
  - Territory application link, contact phone, and legal disclaimer notice.
