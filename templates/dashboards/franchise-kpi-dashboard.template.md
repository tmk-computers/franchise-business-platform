# Franchise Intelligence Dashboard Specification

## 1. Top-Level Metric Tiles
- **Active Franchise Units:** Current Count / Target Quota.
- **Network Gross Customer Billings YTD:** Total Invoiced Volume.
- **Franchisee Commissions Disbursed YTD:** Cumulative Commissions.
- **Blended Network Renewal Rate:** Target $\ge 85\%$.
- **Average Franchise Activation Time:** Target $\le 30$ days.

---

## 2. Analytical Visualizations
1. **Territory Penetration Heatmap:** Geographic display of active, in-onboarding, and open target talukas.
2. **Monthly Billings Waterfall:** Customer Payment -> GST Pass-Through -> 40% Franchisee Share -> 60% Franchisor Retained.
3. **Unit Break-Even Frontier:** Scatter plot of monthly operating overhead vs. monthly sales commission.
4. **Product Mix Contribution:** Donut chart breakdown of revenue per software vertical.
