# Pilot Franchise Launch & Validation Protocol

**Template ID:** `TMP-PLT-001`  
**Purpose:** Reusable operational protocol detailing candidate territory selection, pilot unit onboarding, 30/60/90-day milestone auditing, customer verification surveys, and executive review gates prior to statewide or nationwide franchise rollout.

---

## 1. Pilot Program Objectives
The **Pilot Franchise Launch Protocol** establishes a contained, closely monitored field proof-of-concept phase for `{{CLIENT_NAME}}` to:
1. Validate real-world territory demand, sales cycle length, and pricing elasticity.
2. Pressure-test central deployment engineering, cloud scalability, and Tier-1/2 customer support SLAs.
3. Reconcile empirical franchisee unit cash flows against the theoretical financial model.
4. Refine operations manuals, sales scripts, and training curricula based on live operational feedback.

---

## 2. Pilot Territory Selection Criteria
Pilot locations must be selected based on demographic representation, commercial density, and operational proximity:
- **Territory Count:** Minimum 2 and maximum 4 geographically distinct territories.
- **Demographic Archetypes:**
  - One mature urban/semi-urban commercial center (Tier-2).
  - One developing regional commercial hub (Tier-3/4).
- **Proximity:** Within 2–4 hours travel time from franchisor central engineering office for rapid field assistance.

---

## 3. Pilot Franchisee Incentive & Commercial Framework
- **Early-Bird Commercial Terms:** Reduced franchise joining fee (`{{PILOT_JOINING_FEE}}` vs standard `{{STANDARD_JOINING_FEE}}`) in exchange for intensive reporting obligations and customer discovery participation.
- **Enhanced Central Support:** Dedicated weekly coaching from Senior Business Analysts and priority engineering queue for localized feature enhancements.
- **Mutual Exit Clause:** Pilot agreement provides a structured 90-day review checkpoint with a fee buyback provision if franchisor central SLAs fail to achieve verified standards.

---

## 4. 30 / 60 / 90-Day Milestone Audit Schedule

### 30-Day Milestone: Setup & Initial Outbound Velocity
- Office facility operational with certified branding, workstations, and high-speed connectivity.
- Local marketing launch executed; minimum 50 qualified prospect accounts logged in CRM with deal registration locks.
- Minimum 15 live product demonstrations completed.
- *Audit Gate:* Zero critical compliance violations; initial sales pipeline verified.

### 60-Day Milestone: Commercial Conversion & First Customer Deployments
- First 2–3 paying client contracts executed and invoiced.
- Central engineering deployment executed within published SLA (< 5 business days).
- Net revenue collected, 18% GST segregated, and first commission split disbursed cleanly.
- First-line customer support ticketing operational.
- *Audit Gate:* Revenue reconciliation confirmed by Finance Director.

### 90-Day Milestone: Cash Flow Inflection & Customer Satisfaction Audit
- Cumulative client portfolio reaches 6–8 active contracts.
- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) survey administered to all pilot clients (Target $\ge 85\%$).
- Franchisee operational P&L audited: monthly operating cash flow reaches or approaches unit break-even.
- *Audit Gate:* Comprehensive Pilot Performance Report presented to Executive Committee for **Gate 6 (National Expansion Approval)**.
