# 10-Day Field Sales Certification Curriculum

## Course Overview
- **Target Audience:** Newly hired dedicated salespeople at local franchise offices.
- **Passing Grade:** 80% on written and live simulation examination on Day 10.

---

## Daily Curriculum Schedule
- **Day 1:** Company History, Product Portfolio Overview, and Customer Personas.
- **Day 2:** Product 1 Deep-Dive: Workflows, Architecture, and Pricing.
- **Day 3:** Product 2 Deep-Dive: Workflows and Invoicing Mechanics.
- **Day 4:** Products 3 & 4 Deep-Dive: Demonstrations and Live Scenarios.
- **Day 5:** Products 5 & 6 Deep-Dive: Cloud and CRM Enablement.
- **Day 6:** Central CRM Training: Lead Logging, Deal Locking, and Quota Tracking.
- **Day 7:** Discovery Call Simulation & Handling Gatekeepers.
- **Day 8:** Mastering the 45-Minute Live Product Demonstration.
- **Day 9:** Objection Handling, Negotiation, and Proposal Generation.
- **Day 10:** Final Certification Examination: Live 30-minute Mock Customer Pitch.
