## 2. The Four-Stage Recruitment Funnel
1. **Discovery Call (15 Minutes):** Assess capital readiness, local territory focus, and business background.
2. **Opportunity Deck Walkthrough (45 Minutes):** Present 10-slide pitch deck and unit break-even metrics.
3. **Disclosure Prospectus & Due Diligence (14-Day Cooling-Off):** Deliver prospectus and risk disclosure; review draft agreement.
4. **Signing & Bootcamp Scheduling:** Collect joining fee and schedule 10-day onboarding bootcamp.

---

## 3. Objection Handling Playbook
- *Objection: "What if local customers in my taluka don't want to buy software?"*
  - **Response:** Institutional pain points (fees, GST, society security) are universal. Closing just 1 to 2 deals per month clears all fixed expenses.
- *Objection: "Can I manage this part-time without an office?"*
  - **Response:** No. Client institutions demand local trust and physical commercial presence. Full-time focus and dedicated sales staffing are non-negotiable quality covenants.
