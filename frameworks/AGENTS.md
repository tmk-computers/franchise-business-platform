## 1. Domain Purpose & Scope
The `frameworks/` directory contains the platform's reusable, industry-agnostic business methodology. It codifies franchise model design, financial feasibility, market research, revenue-sharing mechanics, territory intelligence, investor protection, risk assessment, operations governance, performance analytics, and network expansion.

---

## 2. Specialized Agent Rules for `frameworks/`

1. **Strict Client-Agnostic Design:**
   - NEVER embed client-specific commercial terms, proprietary product names, or client customer identities in `frameworks/`.
   - Examples of prohibited terms in generic frameworks: "TMK IT Solutions", "₹25,000 joining fee", "40:60 split as platform default", or "Haveli Taluka".
   - If a client example is needed for illustration, place it strictly in Section 9 ("Example Application") with explicit labeling as an illustrative case study.
2. **Mandatory 9-Section Framework Architecture:**
   Every framework document in `frameworks/` must contain the following 9 standard sections without exception:
   1. *Purpose*
   2. *Business Questions Addressed*
   3. *Required Inputs*
   4. *Methodology & Theoretical Foundations*
   5. *Calculation Logic & Formulas (where applicable)*
   6. *Expected Outputs*
   7. *Risks and Limitations*
   8. *Acceptance Criteria*
   9. *Example Application (labeled illustrative only)*
3. **Mathematical & Formula Precision:**
   - Always express calculation logic using standard mathematical notation with explicit variable definitions.
   - Segregate statutory taxes from revenue distributions in all formula definitions.
4. **Independent Review Requirement:**
   - Any new or modified framework must be peer-reviewed for cross-industry applicability before merging.
