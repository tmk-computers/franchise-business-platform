# Evidence Collection & Verification Standards

## 1. Principles of Empirical Research
To prevent hallucinations and unverified assumptions from entering financial models and client deliverables:
1. **Tier-1 Authoritative Sources:** Government statistical databases (e.g., UDISE+ for schools, MSME Ministry census, Census of India), official corporate regulatory filings, audited annual reports.
2. **Tier-2 Industry Sources:** Recognized market intelligence firms (Gartner, IDC, Nasscom), audited industry trade publications.
3. **Tier-3 Primary Field Evidence:** Transcribed interviews with institutional decision-makers (principals, business owners) conducted by platform analysts.
4. **Mandatory Logging:** Every cited market size, competitor price point, or adoption metric must be recorded in `analyst-workspace/research-methodology/research-evidence-register.md` with full citation metadata.
