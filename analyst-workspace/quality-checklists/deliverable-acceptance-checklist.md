# Deliverable Acceptance & Client Release Checklist

## Sign-Off Gate for Client Deliverables
Prior to releasing any document, presentation, or financial model to client leadership:

- [ ] **Document Control:** Document version number, author, reviewer, and date clearly labeled.
- [ ] **Status Classification:** Clearly tagged as `[DRAFT]`, `[WORKING DISCUSSION DRAFT]`, or `[FINAL]`.
- [ ] **Mandatory Disclaimers:** Legal drafts contain non-final disclaimers; financial projections state they are not guaranteed returns.
- [ ] **Dual Sign-Off:** Signed off by both Analyst 1 (Strategy) and Analyst 2 (Finance).
- [ ] **Inventory Update:** Logged in `clients/<client>/deliverables/deliverable-inventory.md`.
