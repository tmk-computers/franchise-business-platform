## 2. Why the Academy Exists

Traditional franchise sales collateral suffers from severe structural flaws:
1. **Misleading Marketing Claims:** Brochures claim "earn ₹1 Lakh per month from Day 1" while hiding mandatory royalty deductions, local marketing fees, and working capital deficits.
2. **Ambiguous Roles:** Investors purchase a "FOFO" franchise believing it to be a passive investment, only to discover they must work 14 hours a day managing staff and inventory.
3. **Predatory Agreements:** Unilateral contracts allow franchisors to terminate agreements with 24 hours notice while prohibiting franchisees from operating in the same industry for 5 years.
4. **Poor Financial Preparation:** First-time buyers commit 100% of their savings to upfront CapEx, leaving zero working capital to survive the initial 6-month break-even curve.

The Academy counters these traps with transparent financial calculations, empirical research, and rigorous due diligence checklists.
