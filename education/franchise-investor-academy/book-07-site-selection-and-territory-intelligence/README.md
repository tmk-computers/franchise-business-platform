## About This Book

The retail axiom *"Location, Location, Location"* applies acutely to physical franchise businesses. A mediocre brand in an exceptional, high-footfall location will often generate healthy operating cash flows, while an exceptional brand in a compromised, invisible location with poor vehicular access will burn through working capital and fail.

**Site Selection, Territory Intelligence & Real Estate** provides prospective franchise investors with the field-tested tools needed to evaluate physical commercial sites. It teaches you how to map primary, secondary, and tertiary catchment zones, conduct manual pedestrian footfall counts across peak trading hours, negotiate favorable commercial lease terms (rent escalations, CAM charges, lock-in periods, and fit-out rent-free periods), and define clear territory boundaries to prevent franchisor encroachment.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon reading this book, you will be able to:
1. **Map Trade Catchment Zones:** Define and evaluate primary (0–1.5 km), secondary (1.5–3 km), and tertiary (3–5 km) customer demographic zones.
2. **Execute Quantitative Footfall Audits:** Conduct manual pedestrian and vehicular traffic counts across weekday and weekend dayparts.
3. **Negotiate Commercial Leases:** Secure 30–60 day rent-free fit-out periods, cap annual Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges, and limit lease lock-in penalties.
4. **Draft Anti-Encroachment Clauses:** Define enforceable territorial exclusivity radiuses and dark store carve-outs.
5. **Calculate Rent-to-Revenue Thresholds:** Ensure occupancy costs remain below sustainable vertical thresholds (e.g., <15% of net sales in F&B).
