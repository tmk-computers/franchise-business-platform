## About This Book

Every industry vertical operates under fundamentally different economic laws. A Food & Beverage (F&B) franchise features high gross margins (65% to 75%) but suffers from perishable inventory, high labor churn, and low ticket sizes. A Fashion Retail franchise features higher ticket sizes and durable inventory, but ties up massive capital in seasonal unsold stock and markdown risks. An Education or Pre-School franchise offers predictable recurring annual tuition, but faces intense hyper-local reputation sensitivity and strict academic calendar seasonality.

**Sector-by-Sector Franchise Economics** provides an objective financial comparison across India's primary franchising sectors: Quick-Service Restaurants (QSR) & Cafes, Apparel & Consumer Retail, Education & Pre-Schools, Healthcare Diagnostics & Wellness, and IT/Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) Distribution. It breaks down typical CapEx ranges, gross margin structures, working capital velocity, break-even timelines, and failure modes for each industry.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon reading this book, you will be able to:
1. **Compare Vertical Unit Economics:** Evaluate CapEx, gross margins, EBITDA, and working capital cycles across 5 core sectors.
2. **Understand Industry-Specific Risks:** Identify perishable spoilage in F&B, inventory obsolescence in Retail, and fee collection cycles in Education.
3. **Analyze Regulatory Barriers:** Map municipal health licenses, fire NOCs, clinical establishment rules, and educational accreditations.
4. **Size Working Capital by Vertical:** Calculate inventory turnover days and receivables buffers required for each industry.
5. **Select the Right Industry:** Align your capital base, network contacts, and management style with the optimal vertical.
