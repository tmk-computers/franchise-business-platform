## About This Book

Every business investment must have a defined exit strategy. Yet the vast majority of first-time franchise investors enter into long-term commercial agreements without ever contemplating how they will exit the business, realize capital gains, or transfer equity to a successor. Unlike an independent business where the founder can sell shares or assets to any willing buyer, selling a franchised business requires navigating contractual restrictions: **Franchisor Transfer Approvals**, substantial **Transfer Fees**, **Mandatory Refurbishment Covenants**, and the franchisor’s **Right of First Refusal (ROFR)**.

**Franchise Exits, Resales & Succession Planning** is the comprehensive guidebook for extracting maximum value from your franchise enterprise. It teaches you how to calculate business enterprise value using Seller’s Discretionary Earnings (SDE) and EBITDA valuation multiples, structure asset versus stock purchase transactions, negotiate franchisor transfer consents, and establish orderly family succession protocols.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon reading this book, you will be able to:
1. **Value a Franchise Resale:** Apply Seller’s Discretionary Earnings (SDE) and EBITDA multiples to determine fair market enterprise value.
2. **Navigate Franchisor Transfer Consents:** Meet franchisor buyer qualification criteria, transfer fee caps, and training requirements.
3. **Decipher the Right of First Refusal (ROFR):** Understand how franchisor ROFR clauses impact deal timelines and buyer negotiations.
4. **Structure Asset vs. Share Purchases:** Optimize tax treatment (Capital Gains vs Business Income) and insulate incoming buyers from past liabilities.
5. **Plan Generational Succession:** Structure holding company equity, operating deeds, and family governance to ensure smooth business continuity.
