## 1. Instructions & Evaluation Methodology

Before transferring any initial franchise fee, signing a letter of intent, or committing commercial lease capital, complete this 100-point audit.

### How to Score the Opportunity
- **Points per Item:** Award **1 point** if the franchisor provides verified, documentary proof.
- **0 Points:** Award **0 points** if the franchisor provides vague verbal claims, evasive answers, or refuses disclosure.
- **Automatic Deal-Breaker:** Any affirmative check under the **Red Flag Disqualifiers** immediately halts negotiations regardless of the overall numerical score.

### Decision Benchmark Matrix
| Total Audit Score | Viability Classification | Recommended Investor Action |
|---|---|---|
| **90 – 100 Points** | **Institutional Grade** | Proceed to final legal agreement audit by independent attorney. |
| **75 – 89 Points** | **Moderate Risk (Defensible)** | Request contractual amendments and escrow protections for missing items. |
| **50 – 74 Points** | **High Risk (Speculative)** | High likelihood of capital impairment; do not commit without senior counsel. |
| **Below 50 Points** | **Unviable / High Failure Risk** | **REJECT OPPORTUNITY IMMEDIATELY.** Significant risk of total capital loss. |

## 2. The 100-Point Audit Checklist

### Section 1: Franchisor Corporate Background, Leadership & Integrity (Items 1–15)
- [ ] 01. Certificate of Incorporation / LLP Registration verified on Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal.
- [ ] 02. Active DIN (Director Identification Numbers) checked for all directors with zero disqualifications.
- [ ] 03. At least 3 years of audited balance sheets and P&L statements provided and verified.
- [ ] 04. Franchisor is solvent with positive net worth and manageable debt-to-equity ratio (< 1.5).
- [ ] 05. Formal litigation search conducted across Indian e-Courts portal revealing zero fraud or insolvency cases.
- [ ] 06. Executive leadership team has minimum 5+ years of demonstrable operational experience in this industry.
- [ ] 07. Clear organizational chart provided showing dedicated franchise support personnel.
- [ ] 08. Franchisor operates at least 1 profitable company-owned flagship unit (COCO) for > 24 months.
- [ ] 09. Corporate headquarters visit completed; verified physical office and active operational infrastructure.
- [ ] 10. Trademark ownership verified on Indian IP Office portal (Status: "Registered" in appropriate classes).
- [ ] 11. No history of sudden, unexplained corporate rebranding or restructuring in the past 5 years.
- [ ] 12. Corporate bank account statement provided confirming active operations with a scheduled commercial bank.
- [ ] 13. Brand has documented, verifiable customer reviews across Google, Trustpilot, or industry portals (> 4.0 rating).
- [ ] 14. Clear disclosure of parent company, sister companies, and beneficial ownership provided.
- [ ] 15. Clean credit rating report (CIBIL Commercial / CRISIL SME) provided by franchisor.

### Section 2: Business Model, Unit Economics & Financial Transparency (Items 16–30)
- [ ] 16. Comprehensive itemized CapEx breakdown provided with zero hidden or vague "miscellaneous" buffers.
- [ ] 17. Sunk costs (initial franchise fee) clearly separated from recoverable assets (interior fixtures, hardware).
- [ ] 18. Written disclosure of statutory taxes (18% GST) segregated before revenue share calculation.
- [ ] 19. Itemized monthly fixed operating expense (OpEx) model provided (rent, salaries, electricity, SaaS).
- [ ] 20. Operational break-even sales volume calculated and verified using conservative contribution margins.
- [ ] 21. Realistic 36-month pro-forma P&L projection provided with conservative, base, and optimistic scenarios.
- [ ] 22. Simple payback period modeled between 14 and 28 months based on realistic sales velocities.
- [ ] 23. Working capital cushion explicitly budgeted for 3 to 6 months of zero-velocity operational survival.
- [ ] 24. Full disclosure of all ongoing fees: royalties, central marketing levies, tech license fees, audit fees.
- [ ] 25. Supply chain markup policy disclosed in writing (franchisor profit margin on raw materials / inventory).
- [ ] 26. Zero promises of "guaranteed returns," "assured monthly payout," or "risk-free capital."
- [ ] 27. Average transaction size (Average Order Value / Basket Size) verified through actual store receipts.
- [ ] 28. Seasonality factors explicitly modeled in cash flow projections (e.g. holiday peaks, monsoon troughs).
- [ ] 29. Payment gateway and customer collection settlement cycle documented (T+1 or T+2 settlement).
- [ ] 30. Disclosed working capital drawdown J-curve reviewed and stress-tested by an independent accountant.

### Section 3: Site Selection, Catchment & Territory Intelligence (Items 31–45)
- [ ] 31. Formal site selection criteria document provided (square footage, frontage, power, parking).
- [ ] 32. Demographic catchment analysis conducted verifying target population density and disposable income.
- [ ] 33. Exclusive geographic territory precisely defined (Taluka boundary, PIN code list, or defined km radius).
- [ ] 34. Contractual anti-encroachment clause strictly prohibits franchisor opening within protected territory.
- [ ] 35. Online order attribution policy clearly defines revenue split for digital sales in the territory.
- [ ] 36. Direct footfall counting study completed over 7 consecutive days (including weekdays and weekends).
- [ ] 37. Nearby direct competitors mapped and priced within a 3-kilometer radius.
- [ ] 38. Municipal zoning permissions and commercial usage clearance verified for proposed premises.
- [ ] 39. Commercial lease terms reviewed: minimum 3-year lock-in protection with clear escalation caps (< 5% p.a.).
- [ ] 40. Common Area Maintenance (CAM) charges and property taxes verified with landlord.
- [ ] 41. Landlord legal title and ownership deed inspected by independent legal counsel.
- [ ] 42. Utility feasibility confirmed (3-phase electric supply, backup power generator, commercial water line).
- [ ] 43. Fire safety clearance (NOC) and local trade license feasibility audited with municipal authorities.
- [ ] 44. Signage permissions and facade visibility confirmed with local municipal bylaws.
- [ ] 45. Deal registration lock mechanism provided for B2B/software sales (90-day lead exclusivity).

### Section 4: Legal Contract, Restrictive Covenants & Terms (Items 46–60)
- [ ] 46. Complete draft franchise agreement provided at least 14 days prior to signing.
- [ ] 47. Initial contract term is at least 3 to 5 years, providing sufficient runway to achieve payback.
- [ ] 48. Renewal terms clearly defined with pre-negotiated fee caps (renewal fee <= 20% of initial fee).
- [ ] 49. Termination clauses balanced: franchisor cannot terminate without written notice and 30-day cure period.
- [ ] 50. Franchisee has explicit contractual right to terminate if franchisor fails to deliver support or product.
- [ ] 51. Non-compete covenants are reasonable in duration (<= 1 year post-termination) and geographic scope.
- [ ] 52. Trademark license scope explicitly grants legal right to use brand logos and collateral.
- [ ] 53. Intellectual property ownership of client data clearly defined (franchisee owns customer relationships).
- [ ] 54. Dispute resolution mechanism mandates fast-track commercial arbitration in an accessible jurisdiction.
- [ ] 55. Audit rights bounded: franchisor audit notices require 7 days advance written warning.
- [ ] 56. Limitation of liability clauses are reciprocal between franchisor and franchisee.
- [ ] 57. Indemnification protections provided to franchisee for third-party IP infringement claims.
- [ ] 58. Clear liquidated damages clauses with reasonable compensation limits rather than punitive penalties.
- [ ] 59. Force Majeure clause explicitly covers pandemics, natural disasters, and statutory regulatory lockdowns.
- [ ] 60. Agreement reviewed and redlined by an independent corporate attorney specializing in franchising.

### Section 5: Technology Infrastructure, Systems & Support (Items 61–75)
- [ ] 61. Centralized ERP / POS / CRM system provided with demonstrated live uptime > 99.5%.
- [ ] 62. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforces strict data isolation between franchise units.
- [ ] 63. Central helpdesk ticketing system active with defined Service Level Agreements (L1 response < 4 hrs).
- [ ] 64. Dedicated escalation matrix provided with names, phone numbers, and email addresses of senior leads.
- [ ] 65. Comprehensive Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) handbook provided covering all operational workflows.
- [ ] 66. Comprehensive 5-day initial onboarding and training curriculum provided for franchisee and core staff.
- [ ] 67. Ongoing training modules, refresher courses, and sales playbooks accessible via LMS or digital portal.
- [ ] 68. Central marketing calendar provided detailing national brand campaigns funded by central levies.
- [ ] 69. High-resolution localized marketing collateral templates provided (brochures, digital ads, banners).
- [ ] 70. Product demonstration sandbox / demo software environment provided for sales testing.
- [ ] 71. Product warranty and defective merchandise return policy documented with zero franchisee loss.
- [ ] 72. Central supply chain guarantees maximum delivery lead times for inventory replenishment.
- [ ] 73. Field support representative scheduled for minimum quarterly on-site operational reviews.
- [ ] 74. Customer data security protocols comply with applicable Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
- [ ] 75. Automated reporting dashboard displays daily sales, gross margins, and inventory levels in real-time.

### Section 6: Existing Franchisee Interviews & Network Health (Items 76–90)
- [ ] 76. Franchisor provided complete contact directory of ALL active franchisees without cherry-picking.
- [ ] 77. Minimum 5 active franchisees interviewed privately without franchisor representatives present.
- [ ] 78. Interviewed franchisees confirm franchisor delivers promised training and marketing support.
- [ ] 79. Interviewed franchisees confirm actual CapEx matched initial franchisor estimates within 10%.
- [ ] 80. Interviewed franchisees confirm actual break-even timelines matched commercial projections.
- [ ] 81. Interviewed franchisees confirm software/ERP system functions smoothly without chronic outages.
- [ ] 82. Interviewed franchisees confirm customer product quality meets market expectations.
- [ ] 83. Contact directory of former / terminated franchisees requested and audited.
- [ ] 84. At least 2 former franchisees interviewed to understand root causes of operational failure or exit.
- [ ] 85. Annual network turnover rate is low (< 10% of total units closed or transferred annually).
- [ ] 86. Mystery shopping inspection conducted at 3 existing franchise units during peak hours.
- [ ] 87. Existing franchisees report fair and transparent revenue settlement and timely commission payouts.
- [ ] 88. Existing franchisees report constructive dispute resolution during operational disagreements.
- [ ] 89. Franchisees report positive customer repeat purchase rates and strong local referral loops.
- [ ] 90. Majority of surveyed franchisees state they would make the same investment decision again.

### Section 7: Exit Strategy, Resale Rights & Succession Planning (Items 91–100)
- [ ] 91. Contractual right to sell / transfer franchise unit to an approved third-party buyer clearly permitted.
- [ ] 92. Franchisor transfer fee is reasonable and capped (<= 25% of prevailing initial franchise fee).
- [ ] 93. Franchisor Right of First Refusal (ROFR) has strict timeline limit (<= 30 days to match bona fide offer).
- [ ] 94. Clear unit valuation framework provided for going-concern sales based on audited EBITDA multiples.
- [ ] 95. De-branding protocol upon contract expiration is clear and does not impose punitive demolition costs.
- [ ] 96. Family succession / inheritance rights guaranteed in the event of franchisee death or permanent incapacity.
- [ ] 97. Customer contracts and ongoing recurring revenue streams transition smoothly upon approved sale.
- [ ] 98. Equipment buy-back policy defined if franchisor terminates unit without franchisee fault.
- [ ] 99. Security deposit refund protocol guarantees full return within 45 days post-audit upon clean exit.
- [ ] 100. Formal sign-off completed by investor, accountant, and attorney before remitting funds.

## 3. Red Flag Disqualifiers (Automatic Rejection)

If ANY of the following 10 conditions are true, **immediately cease negotiations**:

| Flag ID | Fatal Red Flag Condition | Risk Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| `FLG-01` | **Guaranteed Profit Promises:** Franchisor offers "guaranteed 25% monthly return" or "risk-free income." | High probability of Ponzi/fraud structure; illegal under Indian law. |
| `FLG-02` | **Refusal to Share Franchisee Contacts:** Franchisor refuses to provide uncurated list of active franchisees. | Severe concealment of network-wide financial failure. |
| `FLG-03` | **High Pressure Signing Tactics:** "Sign today or the territory is given to someone else tomorrow." | Coercive sales behavior designed to bypass legal and financial audit. |
| `FLG-04` | **Active Fraud / Cheating Litigation:** Directors have pending criminal charges under IPC 420 or IBC insolvency. | Imminent asset freeze or enterprise collapse. |
| `FLG-05` | **Unregistered / Disputed Trademark:** Brand trademark is rejected, objected, or owned by an unrelated entity. | Franchisee risks immediate trademark infringement injunction and closure. |
| `FLG-06` | **Zero Operating Units:** Franchisor has never operated a profitable unit and has 0 pilot locations. | Investor is being used as an uncompensated Guinea pig for an unproven concept. |
| `FLG-07` | **Undisclosed Supply Markups:** Franchisor mandates purchasing unbranded consumables at 300% market rate. | Predatory margin extraction making franchisee unit profitability impossible. |
| `FLG-08` | **Cash-Only / Off-the-Record Payments:** Franchisor demands joining fees in cash without formal GST tax invoice. | Severe statutory tax evasion exposing franchisee to tax authority raids. |
| `FLG-09` | **Vague / Open-Ended CapEx Estimates:** "Fit-out costs will depend on actuals during construction." | Uncontrolled CapEx blowouts consuming entire working capital reserve. |
| `FLG-10` | **Asymmetric One-Way Agreement:** Franchisor can terminate at will with 24 hours notice; franchisee has 0 rights. | Complete loss of capital investment at franchisor's unilateral discretion. |
