## About This Book

Profit is an accounting concept; cash is the oxygen that keeps an operating business alive. An accounting P&L can show an attractive monthly paper profit of ₹1,50,000, yet the business can be days away from closing its doors because customer payments are locked in credit terms, sales tax liabilities are due tomorrow, and inventory suppliers refuse to deliver raw materials without advance payment.

**Working Capital Management & Cash Flow Survival** provides advanced financial management methodologies tailored to franchise operations. It dissects the **Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)**, teaches operators how to negotiate vendor payment credit without harming brand relationships, models payroll and statutory reserve accounts, and equips operators to survive extended footfall recessions or local market disruptions.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon reading this book, you will be able to:
1. **Calculate the Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC):** Measure the exact days required to convert raw materials and cash outlays back into liquid cash.
2. **Navigate the Initial J-Curve:** Forecast cumulative cash deficits during months 1 to 9 and maintain adequate liquidity buffers.
3. **Structure Bank Account Architecture:** Implement the 3-Account cash management system (Tax Escrow, OpEx Operating, Owner Reserve).
4. **Negotiate Trade Credit Terms:** Transition from cash-on-delivery (COD) to 15-day or 30-day supplier credit terms without personal guarantees.
5. **Survive Cash Flow Shocks:** Execute emergency liquidity protocols when external shocks or construction work outside your store cut footfalls by 50%.
