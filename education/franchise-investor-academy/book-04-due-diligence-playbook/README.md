## About This Book

Most franchise failures in India occur because prospective buyers conduct superficial due diligence—relying on glossy corporate brochures, staged showroom visits, and sales executive promises. In an unregulated franchise market where franchisors are not legally mandated to provide a standardized Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD), the burden of investigation rests entirely on the prospective investor.

**The 100-Point Franchise Due Diligence Playbook** provides a step-by-step forensic investigation protocol. It teaches investors how to inspect corporate registry filings on the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) portal, search litigation records on the national e-Courts platform, audit trademark registrations on IP India, conduct candid mystery shopping at operational units, and interview active and exited franchisees to uncover true unit profitability.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon reading this book, you will be able to:
1. **Audit Corporate Legitimacy:** Verify the legal existence, paid-up capital, charge registers, and director histories of the franchisor entity via MCA-21.
2. **Search Litigation Databases:** Identify ongoing consumer, civil, labor, and NCLT insolvency proceedings using public court portals.
3. **Verify Trademark Ownership:** Confirm that the brand name and logo are registered under Class 35/43 and owned directly by the entity signing the contract.
4. **Conduct Franchisee Validation Calls:** Ask targeted, revealing questions to existing and former franchisees to calculate true churn rates and earnings.
5. **Execute Undercover Mystery Shopping:** Audit real store operations, speed of service, product quality, POS compliance, and staff morale.
