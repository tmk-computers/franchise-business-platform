# Client Confidentiality & Ethical Research Guidelines

## 1. Principles of Commercial Integrity

As analysts and architects modeling franchise ecosystems, our research and models shape substantial capital investments by prospective business owners. We adhere to the highest standard of ethical conduct and confidentiality:

1. **Truth in Projections:** Never fabricate revenue claims, customer adoption metrics, or profit margins to make a franchise opportunity appear artificially attractive.
2. **Duty of Care to Prospective Franchisees:** Financial models must disclose working capital requirements, realistic breakeven timetables, tax liabilities, and downside scenarios.
3. **Protection of Client Trade Secrets:** Product architectural secrets, unannounced features, and internal client cost structures must never be exposed outside authorized client boundaries.

---

## 2. Practical Data Handling Rules for Analysts

### 2.1. Handling Client Commercial Terms
- When working in `clients/tmk-it-solutions/` or any future client directory, verify whether commercial terms (e.g., promotional ₹25,000 joining fee or 40:60 split) are **Approved Policy** or **Proposed Working Assumptions**.
- Working assumptions must be labeled clearly as `[PROPOSED]` or `[WORKING HYPOTHESIS]` in all presentations and models until formally ratified in `clients/<client>/approvals/approval-matrix.md`.

### 2.2. Competitor Intelligence Ethics
- When researching competitors for the 6 software products (e.g., competing School ERP or CRM systems), gather intelligence only through legitimate public sources (official websites, public pricing tables, whitepapers, industry reports).
- Never misrepresent identity or breach terms of service when gathering comparative data.
- Cite all external market data sources in `analyst-workspace/research-methodology/research-evidence-register.md`.

### 2.3. Deliverable Versioning and Preservation
- Never overwrite or delete approved deliverables in `clients/<client>/deliverables/`.
- Superseded versions must be archived with timestamp tags (e.g., `model_v1.0_20261026_archived.xlsx`).
